## Reference behavior (source-derived)

### A. The Arcane Pattern Crafter

1. **One block, one tile, one dial — and no inventory and no GUI of its own.** The block wires the tile, takes the iron material and metal sound, and is named "Arcane Pattern Crafter" (`common/blocks/crafting/BlockPatternCrafter.java:44`, `:48-52`; `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:311`). Placement records the placing player's horizontal facing without the base's sneak inversion (`common/blocks/crafting/BlockPatternCrafter.java:75-80`; the base's own placement and enabled-state machinery, including its sneak inversion, `common/blocks/BlockTCDevice.java:27-29`, `:54-66`, `:91-115`). The tile exposes exactly one traceable cuboid — the dial — which the facing math places on the face opposite the recorded facing, so the dial starts toward the placing player (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:274-277`, `:279-294`), and both the selection box and the client highlight agree with that cuboid (`common/blocks/crafting/BlockPatternCrafter.java:98-111`, `:117-123`). A right-click that ray-traces to the dial cycles the pattern server-side with a click sound, and no other interaction is offered (`:82-96`). The block drops itself independently of the dial setting (`:54-57`), and it appears once on the mod's creative tab (the block base sets the tab `common/blocks/BlockTC.java:20`). Registration: block `common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:340`, tile `:411`; the device is built from a fifty-vis arcane workbench recipe under the `ARCANEPATTERNCRAFTER` research key, whose entry sits in the artifice category behind the dioptra and lists that recipe in its second stage (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:255`; `assets/thaumcraft/research/artifice.json:260-275`). The client-side dial render draws from its own textures, and the renderer's bore-base model field is instantiated and never read — another dead field for item 16's list (`client/renderers/tile/TilePatternCrafterRenderer.java:15-25`; the visual layer is `CLI-02`).

2. **The dial selects one of ten fixed cell masks over a 3x3 grid, and every filled cell is fed the same single item type.** The cycle walks type zero through nine and wraps (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:251-258`). The masks, in dial order: the full nine-cell square, a single cell, two cells across, two cells down, a two-by-two block, three across, three down, six cells in two rows, six cells in two columns, and an eight-cell ring — and the item count a craft consumes is exactly the number of filled cells, nine, one, two, two, four, three, three, six, six, eight (`:95-128` for the counts, `:163-231` for the mask fills). The research text walks the same idea in player terms — the same item repeated in a shape, with two planks to a stick and an ingot to nine nuggets as the examples (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1544`).

3. **The crafting contract: extract from the block above, match a vanilla 3x3 recipe, deposit output plus container remainders below — all-or-nothing, once per second.** The tile ticks an internal counter and runs its work only every twentieth pass and only while the block is enabled (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:91`). It resolves an item handler above it from that block's bottom face and one below from that block's top face, and does nothing at all if either side has no handler (`:129-131`). For each slot of the upper inventory it takes the slot's stack, rewrites a copy to the pattern's count, and demands that a simulated extraction of that exact count succeed through the ore-tolerant item filter (`:132-136`; the filter and match routine `api/ThaumcraftInvHelper.java:26-46`, `common/lib/utils/InventoryUtils.java:431-470`, and the extraction walk `:123-150`). On a matching stack it builds the mask in a private 3x3 matrix, asks the vanilla crafting registry for the recipe, and computes the result plus container remainders (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:232-237`). The commit gate requires power of at least one and a simulated insertion of the result into the lower handler to come back empty, and each remainder must also fit (`:136-143`). Only then does it insert the result for real, then the remainders, then extract the inputs for real, fire the block event, spend one power, and stop for this pass (`:144-155`). A failure at any gate moves the scan to the next slot, so a stacked inventory can still find one craftable item per second (`:132-158`).

4. **Vis and redstone: bank up to five per top-up, spend one per craft, stop when powered.** Whenever the power figure reaches zero the pass may draw up to five vis from the aura, banking it (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:92-94`; the drain API `api/aura/AuraHelper.java:19-21` and its handler `common/world/aura/AuraHandler.java:120-133`, which caps the drain at what the chunk holds and answers with what was actually taken). Every committed craft spends exactly one (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:153`), matching the research text's one vis per operation (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1545`). A redstone signal never reaches this tile directly: the shared device base mirrors the block's powered state into the enabled property on placement and on every neighbour change (`common/blocks/BlockTCDevice.java:44-52`, `:68-75`), the property lives in the metadata's top bit (`common/lib/utils/BlockStateUtils.java:28-30`, `:20-22`), and the tile's work gate reads that bit — so powering the block pauses both the drain and the crafting (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:91`).

5. **What the crafter persists, and what clients see.** The power figure is written and read in the tile's own save hooks (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:65-75`), and the dial setting rides the sync hooks — which the tile base also uses as the save form and as the block-update payload (`:54-63`; `common/tiles/TileThaumcraft.java:42-52`, `:50-54`, `:64-74`). A dial cycle syncs the block and marks the tile dirty (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:256-257`); the power figure moves without a dirty mark. The only per-craft wire traffic is the block event that triggers the client dial animation and its clacks (`:152`, `:260-268`, with the client spin `:78-90`). The tick counter that phases the one-second cadence starts from a random seed and is never written (`:33`, `:43`), and the real inventory state lives entirely in the two neighbouring handlers.

6. **The crafter's dupe-safety surfaces.** Every commit is a single server-tick sequence of simulate-then-commit (finding 3), so the interesting cases are the ones a rank-1 run can break: a full or absent lower handler blocks extraction entirely — the simulated output insertion comes back non-empty before anything moves (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:136`); a thin upper inventory — fewer than the pattern count — fails the simulated extraction and nothing happens; and the two real insertions ignore their leftover returns (`:144-149`), so a handler whose real insertion disagrees with its simulation is the loss path to probe, not a duplication path. Breaking the block destroys the banked power and the dial setting with the tile; reloading the chunk leaves both intact (finding 5). The block's break drop is the block item, meta-cleared, and no item of progress travels with it (`common/blocks/crafting/BlockPatternCrafter.java:54-57`).

### B. The Arcane Bore

7. **A construct, not a block — placed by the shared turret placer item and owned on placement.** The Bore is an owned construct with fifty health and a follow range of thirty-two (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:69`, `:118-122`). The placer item carries three variants — the basic crossbow, the advanced crossbow, and the Bore (`common/entities/construct/ItemTurretPlacer.java:20`; names `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:205-207`). Placement refuses the underside, targets the clicked cell when replaceable and the neighbouring cell otherwise, refuses when the two-block column is obstructed, clears both cells, spawns the Bore along the placing player's horizontal look direction, stamps owned, valid-spawn, and owner identity, plays a placement sound, and then shrinks the stack unconditionally — the reference does not exempt creative here, unlike the golem placer (`common/entities/construct/ItemTurretPlacer.java:23-47`, `:48-62`, `:59`, `:63-71`). The owner's sneak right-click drops the held pickaxe and a fresh Bore placer back into the world and removes the entity; the owner's plain right-click opens the screen (finding 12) (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:440-458`). Any hit from the owner that is not from below re-faces the device toward the attacker and deals nothing; other attackers hurt it normally (`:397-411`). Death drops the held pickaxe, and the loot roll is the Bore's own drop-table override (`:425-437`, `:522-545`). The base construct's ownership, despawn, and team rules are the shared ones already derived in [aut-02-evidence.md](aut-02-evidence.md) items 7 and 20. Registration: entity `common/config/ConfigEntities.java:100`; item `common/config/ConfigItems.java:210`.

8. **Activation is a redstone-at-the-base or rail-underfoot question, and off means eyes forward and no target.** Once per tick the server side reads the block under the entity, resolves a rail underfoot if one is there, and then: on the mod's Arcane Activator Rail the active flag is the inverse of the rail's powered state — a powered rail switches the Bore off, an unpowered one switches it on; on any other surface while not riding, the active flag is simply the redstone power read at the cell beneath the entity — and riding a rail that is not the mod's leaves the flag unchanged (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:135-151`). Inactive, the Bore clears any pending target and stares straight ahead (`:159-162`); with no target to dig it looks two blocks ahead at eye level (`:180-182`). The activation flag itself is synced entity data, so clients see it flip (`:467-472`).

9. **The digging loop: ray-cast a target, spiral for the next one, harvest with a fake player, and eject into a neighbour.** Work requires a pickaxe in the hand slot that is not one point from breaking (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:186-192`). With no target and active, the search ray-casts from the previous target position along the facing for radius times eight blocks and takes the first harvestable hit that is not its own cell or the cell beneath it, computing a per-block delay from the block's hardness and the pickaxe's speed (`:337-370`). When the ray finds nothing, a spiral walk of increasing radius, centered one step ahead of the Bore at eye height, produces the next cell to probe (`:372-393`). The size figures: the radius is the held pickaxe's enchantability divided by three plus twice the DESTRUCTIVE infusion level, with the sum floored at two (`:194-201`; the infusion levels read are DESTRUCTIVE, BURROWING, SOUNDING, and REFINING `common/lib/enchantment/EnumInfusionEnchantment.java:15-18`); depth is eight times that radius plus sixteen per BURROWING level (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:203-207`); speed is half the pickaxe's block destroy speed plus efficiency (`:219-226`); fortune is the greater of the pickaxe's fortune and its SOUNDING infusion level, and silk touch disables fortune (`:209-217`, `:236-242`, zeroed at `:268-271`). Each dig synthesizes a fake player named for the Bore, hands it the pickaxe, and runs the shared harvest helper, which fires the break event and the harvest for that player (`:272-278`; `common/lib/utils/BlockUtils.java:81-130`, with the creative and enchantment-override branches at `:97-101` and `:103-130`). The drops route through a static map keyed by the fake player's stashed entity id: the mod's harvest event handler collects the event's drops into that map and clears the event's own list, so no vanilla drop entities spawn for the Bore's breaks (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:244-259`). After the harvest the dig also sweeps any loose item entities within one and a half blocks of the dug cell, copying their stacks into the same list and removing the entities (`:283-289`). REFINING infusion replaces an ore drop with its native cluster through the shared mining-result table at a chance of one-eighth per level plus one, when silk touch is not in play (`:290-296`; `common/lib/utils/Utils.java:112-121`; the default ore-to-cluster entries `common/config/ModConfig.java:268-271`). Ejection tries the six neighbouring cells in the enum's order — down, up, north, south, west, east — and inserts into the first that exposes an item handler, with anything that does not fit left as a dropped item at the Bore's position, aimed behind the device when no handler exists at all (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:297-313`; the ejection helper `common/lib/utils/InventoryUtils.java:101-121`). The pickaxe takes one point of damage per fifty blocks broken (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:315-330`), and the dug cell is then set to air (`:331`).

10. **The charge is the Bore's vis reservoir — refilled ten ticks at a time with no cap — with one tree-text caution.** Every tenth tick, while the charge reads under ten, the Bore drains up to ten vis from the aura and adds the full amount with no clamp (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:132-134`, `:413-415`; the drain handler `common/world/aura/AuraHandler.java:120-133`), so a top-up begun just under ten can leave the balance near twenty — the refill guard, not the add, is what bounds it. A dig spends a quarter charge (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:95`), is refused while the charge is below that, and re-faces the target cell while digging (`:163-165`). Caution: the tree text renders the debit as a cast to a byte (`:166`), and a bytecode read of the shipped jar — `REF-0007` in [reference-catalog.md](reference-catalog.md) — shows that cast is faithful, not a decompiler artifact: the float subtraction truncates through a byte and back on every dig, so every dig re-truncates the balance to a whole number and the effective debit is about one charge per block, while the research text states the intended economy as one vis per four blocks (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1537`). The port must reproduce the truncation, not the intent; T6 settles what the running game actually shows (the same caution class recorded for the golem's fragile arithmetic in [aut-02-evidence.md](aut-02-evidence.md) finding 10).

11. **What the Bore writes, what clients see, and what the drop capture keeps.** The entity's save form carries the charge, the facing index, and the active flag (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:482-496`); the base construct adds its owner and despawn keys as derived in [aut-02-evidence.md](aut-02-evidence.md) finding 8. Two synced data entries exist — facing and active (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:467-472`) — and the charge is a plain field referenced nowhere outside the class, so no client ever sees it (`:506-512`). Status bytes 16 and 17 set the client digging flag used by the renderer (`:566-577`), a purpose-built packet carries the dig target, the Bore's id, and the delay to every player within thirty-two blocks (`common/lib/network/fx/PacketFXBoreDig.java:29-33`, `:64-84`; the send site `common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:173-183`), and a digging rumble plays on a timer (`:167-170`). The drop map is a static, per-JVM table keyed by entity id, refilled and emptied per harvest (`:579-583`, `:256`, `:316`); nothing persists it and nothing prunes it.

12. **The Bore's screen is one pickaxe slot wired to the entity's hand, plus a readout plate.** The container adds a single slot that reads and writes the entity's main hand, with a stack limit of sixty-four, above a standard player inventory (`common/container/ContainerArcaneBore.java:21-34`; `common/container/slot/SlotArcaneBorePickaxe.java:14-16`; `common/container/slot/SlotMobEquipment.java:18-27`, `:33-35`). It accepts any pickaxe or pickaxe-class tool (`common/container/slot/SlotArcaneBorePickaxe.java:14-16`) and its interaction check always passes, so the screen has no distance gate (`common/container/ContainerArcaneBore.java:44-46`); shift-transfer moves between the slot and the player both ways (`:48-70`). The screen draws a health bar, a warning marker when the held pickaxe is one point from breaking, and a stats plate: width from the radius, depth, speed against stone, and refining, fortune, and silk touch lines — as hardcoded English strings rather than language keys (`client/gui/GuiArcaneBore.java:38-49`, `:50-82`). The screen and its container are registered on gui id fourteen, keyed by entity id (`proxies/ProxyGUI.java:89-90`, `:161-162`), opened from the owner interaction path (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:453`).

13. **Ownership and control from the Bore's side.** Only the owner opens the screen, re-faces by hit, and retrieves the device; a held name tag passes the interaction through to the base construct's handling (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:440-458`; the base-side not-owned and naming behavior [aut-02-evidence.md](aut-02-evidence.md) items 7 and 20). There is no seal-style lock and no colour system on this device family; the only gates between a player and its operation are ownership, redstone, and the pickaxe in its hand.

### C. The shared surfaces

14. **The turret placer item is the shared placement surface, and the Bore's gate is an infusion.** One item carries the basic crossbow, the advanced crossbow, and the Bore as metadata variants (`common/entities/construct/ItemTurretPlacer.java:20`; registration `common/config/ConfigItems.java:210`, declaration `api/items/ItemsTC.java:134`, names `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:205-207`). The two crossbows are arcane workbench recipes under their own research keys, and the Bore is an infusion recipe — a crossbow turret as the catalyst, instability four, with Energy-, Earth-, Mechanism-, Void-, and Motion-flavoured components — under the research key "ARCANEBORE" with the infusion category's own machinery (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:253-254`, `:335`; `assets/thaumcraft/research/artifice.json:277`, `:293`, `:308-321`; the infusion catalog and its registration pass are the shared machinery of [alc-03-evidence.md](alc-03-evidence.md) item 6). That infusion parent is what the workboard's `ALC-03` dependency means in practice: the Bore cannot be built before the infusion matrix works.

15. **The Arcane Activator Rail is a mod block whose only job is the turret family's control.** It is a powered-rail block registered under the mod's namespace with its own name and creative tab presence (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:342`; `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:344`), and the Bore's rail branch keys off exactly that block (finding 8; `common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:143-147`). The crossbow turret carries the same branch with its own effect — a powered rail stops its thinking (`common/entities/construct/EntityTurretCrossbow.java:236-243`). The arcane recipe that produces the mod rail from a vanilla activator rail is early-game and already characterized in the workbench pass ([rsr-02-evidence.md](rsr-02-evidence.md) item 20; `common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:187`).

### D. Dormant and edge paths

16. **Dead plumbing a port must not copy as live behavior.** The Bore declares four fields that are assigned and never read — a pause counter and its maximum, and two beam objects left from an older rendering route (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:74-78`, `:96-100`; the live effects go through the dig packet and the status bytes, finding 11). The Bore container's enchant hook accepts button zero and the progress-bar hook is empty, while the screen declares no buttons that could ever send one (`common/container/ContainerArcaneBore.java:36-38`, `:40-42`; the screen's only widgets are the slot and the player inventory `client/gui/GuiArcaneBore.java:21-27`). The pickaxe slot's own change hook is a no-op above the base that already writes through to the entity (`common/container/slot/SlotArcaneBorePickaxe.java:18-20` versus `common/container/slot/SlotMobEquipment.java:22-28`), and the Bore's knockback dampener caps upward motion without a visible gameplay purpose (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:460-465`). The Pattern Crafter's renderer keeps a bore-base model field that is assigned and never read (`client/renderers/tile/TilePatternCrafterRenderer.java:17`, `:22`).

17. **Edge behaviors worth a capture.** The crafter's scan is slot-order biased — the first slot whose kind can pay the pattern wins, so a chest holding two craftable kinds alternates by slot order, not by recipe value (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePatternCrafter.java:131-158`). Power can hold fractional values because drains return floats, and only whole units are spent (`:92-94`, `:153`). The Bore's ejection order is fixed — down first — so a device flanked by two inventories always feeds the first in enum order (`common/entities/construct/EntityArcaneBore.java:297-313`); its loose-item sweep will absorb any item entity near a dug cell, including one a player just dropped (`:283-289`); horizontal motion is damped to a fifth, which is what keeps it from wandering off its cell (`:514-516`); and the kill command routes through a large damage hit rather than a special path (`:518-520`).

## Multiplayer and divergence classes

18. **What the pair sends.** The Bore registers with sixty-four-block tracking, three-tick updates, and velocity updates (`common/config/ConfigEntities.java:100`); its facing and active flag are synced data, its dig start and stop are status bytes, its dig-progress beam is the dedicated packet inside thirty-two blocks, and its sounds are ordinary entity sounds (finding 11). The crafter's dial setting syncs through the tile update payload on every cycle, and its craft animation through the block event (finding 5). No packet carries the crafter's power bank, and no packet anywhere carries the Bore's charge (findings 5 and 11).

19. **Divergence classes to hunt at rank one.** (a) The dial state of a crafter a second player watches: the cycle syncs, but confirm a second client sees both the setting and the spin on the same cycle. (b) The Bore's charge is invisible to every client, so two clients comparing a starving Bore against a working one will disagree about why — record what the screen shows in each case. (c) The dig fx packet is bounded to thirty-two blocks while tracking is sixty-four, so a distant watcher sees the device move and idle without the beam — record the boundary. (d) An inactive Bore still tops its charge up (finding 10), so a watcher must not read activity from the charge; only the active flag and motion are shared. (e) Both devices price work from the shared aura, so cross-client aura drift belongs to `WLD-01` and shows up here as different stall points on different clients' readouts.

## Rank-1 capture checklist

Reference setup for every step: the clean 1.12.2 profile whose only mods are the BETA26 jar and Baubles (`REF-0002` and the "Environment feasibility" section of [reference-catalog.md](reference-catalog.md)); a fresh creative world with its seed recorded in a new `REF` entry before the session. Both devices are reachable from the mod's creative tab — the crafter block through the block base's tab (`common/blocks/BlockTC.java:20`) and the Bore placer with its three variants (`common/items/ItemTCBase.java:17-20`, `:44-55`). The operator command surface `/thaumcraft research <player> <list|all|reset|<key>>` and `/thaumcraft reload` is the setup lever between measurements and must not be used during one (`common/lib/CommandThaumcraft.java:64-70`, `:84-107`); device behavior itself is not research-gated in the reference — only the recipes to build the devices are, and those gates belong to each recipe's own lane — so no grant is needed before any measurement below. Aura readings use the Thaumometer, whose readout is fed from the chunk's aura (`common/items/tools/ItemThaumometer.java:92-100`). Start every structure from a recorded position and screenshot so the reload steps can compare exact cells.

- **T1 — The crafter block and its dial.** Place the "Arcane Pattern Crafter" from the mod tab with a chest above and a chest below. Right-click the dial and record each of the ten pattern positions in order, the click sound and the dial's spin, and that right-clicking anywhere else on the device never opens anything. Break it and record the block returning plainly, the setting not traveling with the item (items 1-2; bounded to one device).
- **T2 — One craft end-to-end and the vis price.** Stock nine iron ingots in the upper chest with the full nine-cell pattern set; record the upper chest losing nine and the lower chest gaining one iron block about a second apart, then hold the Thaumometer across ten repetitions and record the aura readout falling by ten, or by whatever the same-thread cadence actually delivers — the ten-vis expectation is derived, the figure is not (items 2-4; bounded to ten crafts).
- **T3 — Blocked output, thin input, and the count.** Fill the lower chest so one more iron block cannot fit and record that nothing is drawn; free one output slot and record the craft; stock eight ingots against the nine-cell pattern and record nothing happening; then run thirty crafts and count inputs and outputs exactly (items 3 and 6; bounded to one chest pair, thirty crafts).
- **T4 — The crafter across a reload.** Let the upper chest run empty mid-run so power banks, then save and reload; record the dial pattern surviving, whether the banked power survives, and that no item is duplicated across the reload (items 4-6; bounded to two reloads).
- **T5 — The Bore placed.** Take the Arcane Bore placer from the mod tab. Place it and record: the stack consumed even in creative, the refusal when the two-block column is obstructed, the spawn facing along the placing player's look direction (away from the placing player), the ownership lock (a second player cannot open or retrieve it), the owner's sneak-right-click returning the placer and the held pickaxe, and the owner's left-click re-facing it toward the attacker (item 7; if a second client is unavailable, record the parts that can be observed and mark the rest not-run; bounded to one placement and retrieval).
- **T6 — The Bore digs, and its vis rate (the tree-text caution).** Insert a diamond pickaxe through the screen, redstone-power the block beneath the Bore with a lever, and face it at a stone bank. Record: the start and stop on the input, the digging cadence, the pickaxe losing about one durability per fifty blocks, the drops arriving in an adjacent chest with anything unfittable falling at the Bore, and — explicitly — the aura readout across a fixed block count against both candidate economies: one vis per four blocks (the research text) or one per block (the tree text's byte cast). That single comparison settles finding 10 for the port (items 8-10; bounded to one Bore, two stacks of stone).
- **T7 — The Bore across a reload, on rails.** Mid-dig, save and reload; record charge and facing persistence, digging resuming, and no duplication of the drop held at the moment of the save. Then place the Bore on the mod's Arcane Activator Rail and record the inversion — unpowered rail on, powered rail off — plus the same lever control on a plain block beneath it (items 8, 11, 15; bounded to two reloads).
- **T8 — The dupe sweep (the acceptance slice).** Run both devices in one line — a hopper-fed chest above the crafter, its lower chest feeding a Bore's side chest — and count every item entering and leaving across a fixed run of fifty crafts, with one save and reload and one walk-away chunk unload inside the run. The acceptance "consumes inputs and produces expected outputs without dupes" fails on any count that escapes (items 3, 6, 9, 15; bounded to fifty crafts).
