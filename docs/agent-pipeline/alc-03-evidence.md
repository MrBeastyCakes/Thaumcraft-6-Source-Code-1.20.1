## Reference behavior (source-derived)

### A. The apparatus

1. **The matrix is a tile-backed block with an invisible model.** The block wires the matrix tile in its constructor and declines to render (`common/blocks/crafting/BlockInfusionMatrix.java:14-19`, `:21-35`); it is registered as `infusionMatrix` (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:327`; `api/blocks/BlocksTC.java:144`) and is named the Runic Matrix in the language file (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:305`). The tile is the whole machine: it ticks (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:256`), takes caster interactions, reports itself as an aspect container, and draws its own goggles text (`:78`).
2. **Pedestals each hold exactly one item, and the altar is one center pedestal plus outlying ones.** The pedestal block carries a charge property and implements the stabiliser interface (`common/blocks/devices/BlockPedestal.java:32-43`); the tile is a one-slot inventory with that slot synced and a stack limit of one (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePedestal.java:16-26`); a hand click deposits one held item or drops the held stack (`common/blocks/devices/BlockPedestal.java:57-83`). Three registrations exist — arcane, ancient, eldritch (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:264-266`; `api/blocks/BlocksTC.java:91-93`), named Arcane/Eldritch/Ancient Pedestal (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:376-378`).
3. **The structure is the pedestal below and four pillars diagonally beside it, one level under the matrix, plus optional stones under the pillars.** Validity requires a pedestal directly two blocks below the matrix and pillars at all four diagonal positions of the same level (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:287-289`); the pillars come in arcane, ancient and eldritch variants (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:274-276`; names `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:302-304`); the speed and cost stones are checked one level lower, directly beneath those four pillar positions (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:277-278`, `common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:789-799`; names `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:356-357`).
4. **The stabilizer, the inlay, the candles, and vanilla skulls are the paraphernalia.** The stabilizer block is a stabiliser of 0.25 (`common/blocks/devices/BlockStabilizer.java:19-24`, `:64-72`) backed by an energy tile (`common/tiles/devices/TileStabilizer.java:16-29`); redstone inlay is a stabiliser of 0.025 that also wires the shield, registered at `common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:295` (`common/blocks/devices/BlockInlay.java:40-54`; name `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:353`); all sixteen dyed candles are stabilisers of 0.1 (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:279-281`; `common/blocks/basic/BlockCandle.java:83-100`); and a vanilla skull counts like any stabiliser without an explicit amount (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:722-724`, `:749-756`).

### B. The recipe type and its registry

5. **An infusion recipe is a research key, a central-item ingredient, a component list, an aspect requirement, an output object, and an instability rating.** The type declares those fields (`api/crafting/InfusionRecipe.java:16-24`); the constructor converts the central item and every component through the API helper and throws on any invalid one (`:26-52`); output, aspects, and instability each have a per-player overload so subclasses can compute them (`:71-97`); and the base match test is research known, the central ingredient applied, and an unordered one-to-one component match through Forge's matcher (`:58-64`).
6. **Recipes live in the same shared catalog as every other craft and are added in one config pass.** The registration call stores by resource location (`api/ThaumcraftApi.java:190-193`) into the catalog at `:85-87`, and the pass is `initializeInfusionRecipes` (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:267`), invoked from the registry event along with the other recipe families (`Registrar.java:79`). The shipped instability ratings span zero (the harvest seal among them) through the mid values — the seal-break recipe at 1, the flux crystal cluster at 4, focus upgrades at 3 and 5, the jar brain at 4, the mirror at 1, the essentia mirror at 2, the hand mirror at 5 — up to 6 for the Vis Amulet, with the pass running wider further down its list — 7 for the void siphon and 8 for the three masks, the causality collapser, and the voidseer pearl (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:268-270`, `:277-281`, `:291-293`, `:345-347`, `:368-370`).
7. **Two subclass recipes carry their own numbers.** The infusion enchantment recipe fixes instability at 4, gates on tool, armor, bauble, or rechargable classes and on the enchantment's maximum rank, scales its aspect cost by the new rank plus a third per other infusion enchantment already on the item, and can stamp warp on the result as it is built (`common/lib/crafting/InfusionEnchantmentRecipe.java:32-40`, `:42-117`, `:142-162`, `:119-140`). The runic augment recipe sets instability to 5 plus half the item's current runic count, prices itself at a protection aspect base of 20 plus 20 doubled per existing rank with half that value each of crystal and energy, and grows its own component list by one amber per rank (`common/lib/crafting/InfusionRunicAugmentRecipe.java:23-43`, `:73-77`, `:61-71`, `:79-90`). Both are registered in the same pass (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:282-283`, `:309-311`).

### C. Matching and activation

8. **A caster click is the only switch, and invalid structure silently deactivates.** The caster hands the click to the tile (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:321-329`); on the server an idle matrix with a valid structure activates with a sound, and an active, idle matrix runs the craft start (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:828-845`). Nothing turns an active matrix off except a failed structure check, which runs every hundred ticks while idle and every twenty while crafting (`:266-271`).
9. **The craft start reads the center pedestal and every other pedestal, then asks the catalog.** The catalyst is copied from the pedestal two below (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:299-310`); the components are every non-empty pedestal stack found by the surroundings scan (`:311-321`); at least one component is required (`:322-324`); the first matching catalog entry wins (`:325` with `common/lib/crafting/ThaumcraftCraftingManager.java:116-123`, iterating the same hash map as the `ALC-00` catalog); the recipe's output, instability, and aspects are resolved (`:332-348`) after forcing the cost multiplier up to its 0.5 floor (`:326-328`); and the crafting flag plus a start sound close the call (`:349-354`). No essentia is checked at start, extras on pedestals are ignored, and a matched recipe with an empty essentia supply starts and then stalls (item 25).

### D. Essentia consumption (endpoint only)

10. **The price is the recipe's aspect list scaled by the altar's cost multiplier.** Each aspect is multiplied and floored, amounts that round to zero are dropped, and the result becomes the working requirement (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:341-348`), which is both synced to clients and what the goggles display (`:145-162`, `:938-941`).
11. **The draw is one unit per cycle through the shared transport handler, from any source within twelve blocks.** Each cycle walks the remaining aspects and asks the handler to drain one unit of the first aspect with a positive amount (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:496-505`); the handler takes one unit from the first source in its cached distance-ordered list that is not blocked and yields a unit (`common/lib/events/EssentiaHandler.java:31-62`) — the cache, its ten-second suppression, and the blocked flag are [alc-02-evidence.md](alc-02-evidence.md) item 16 and [fnd-04-evidence.md](fnd-04-evidence.md) item 21 and are not re-derived here; with no direction given, the search is the given range around the matrix's own position as a box whose vertical span runs from twelve below to eleven above — the top layer of a nominal cube is excluded (`common/lib/events/EssentiaHandler.java:210-259`), and the last argument the matrix passes is only a client-effect duration for the drawn trail, carried by the source-effect packet (the `ext` value at `common/lib/events/EssentiaHandler.java:54-56`; `common/lib/network/fx/PacketFXEssentiaSource.java:24-33`, `:47-57`). Jars, alembics, mirrors, or anything else that is a source inside that cube can feed the matrix; tubes are not required. The matrix's own container surface is a display stub — it reports its requirement list and accepts everything while storing nothing (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:938-980`).
12. **A failed draw is itself an instability cost.** Every attempt that finds nothing subtracts 0.25 stability, then the cycle tries the next aspect or ends; a failed pull also marks the surroundings dirty so the altar re-tallies its stabilisers before the next cycle (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:506-511`).

### E. Instability: value, bands, schedule, and events

13. **The value comes from the recipe, the material modifiers, and the paraphernalia, and it moves every cycle.** The rating is taken from the recipe (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:340`); the multipliers and the replenish rate are computed by the surroundings scan (section F); the cap is 25 and the floor is -100 (`:118`, `:366-368`); while active and idle, and not crafting, the value recovers by at least 0.1 each regeneration step — one every five ticks or the cycle delay, whichever is longer (`:272-279`); and the goggles read out the band name, the gain per cycle, and the loss per cycle (`:986-992`), whose strings are `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:595-601`.
14. **Four bands set the per-cycle loss, and each cycle also adds the replenish value.** Very stable above half the cap — integer division puts that boundary at 12 — stable from 0, unstable down to -25, very unstable at or below (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:232-234`), with the band dividing the recipe instability by 5, 6, 7, or 8 to get the loss per cycle (`:236-254`, `:357-359`); the actual loss is a uniform draw up to that value, then the replenish rate is added and the result is clamped (`:363-371`). Cycles run every countDelay ticks while active and crafting (`:280-283`), where countDelay is half the cycle time the scan left behind (a baseline cycle time of 10, so five ticks; `:704`, `:800`).
15. **Every cycle first re-validates the catalyst, then rolls for trouble.** The catalyst check compares the center pedestal's item against the recorded input, honoring the wildcard damage marker and treating the comparison as count-insensitive (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:372-384`; the equality helper is `api/ThaumcraftInvHelper.java:105-123`). A cycle then always rolls one of the twenty-four event entries when the catalyst check failed, and otherwise only while stability is below zero, at a chance of the absolute stability over 1500 (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:385`). A save by a stabilizer (item 22) or a missing event leaves the craft untouched.
16. **The event roll is a fixed twenty-four-way table.** Four entries eject and drop an item from a random loaded pedestal; three add warp; three zap one living target within ten blocks; two zap all of them; two more eject with flux goo; two more eject with aura pollution; one more ejects with goo; one destroys an item in place and pollutes the aura by 5 to 9; two inflict harmful potion effects on one target; one does so to all; two eject with an explosion at the pedestal; and one fires a literal explosion at the matrix itself of radius 1.5 to 2.5 (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:386-447`, `:443-446`). After any event the stability is refunded by 5 to 10, the nearby players are taught the instability note, and the craft survives if the catalyst still stands (`:448-452`); if it does not, the craft ends immediately — flag off, requirement and rating cleared, failure sound (`:454-462`).
17. **The effects, exactly.** Ejection retries up to twenty-five times to find a loaded pedestal; a connected stabilizer can absorb the strike for 5 to 10 of its energy (item 22); otherwise the item is dropped on the pedestal — or destroyed in place for the destroy variant — and the sub-variants add flux goo above the pedestal, 5 to 9 units of aura pollution, or a size-one explosion at the pedestal (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:611-653`). Warp gives a random player within ten blocks one normal warp a quarter of the time, else two to five temporary warp (`:598-609`). Zaps deal 4 to 7 magic damage, to one target or to all (`:552-563`). The harmful pair is flux taint for 120 ticks or an incurable vis-exhaust for 2400 (`:565-582`). The reward side of the event is the same for all: stability returns upward.
18. **The client sees the show, the goggles see the numbers.** While crafting the client runs the rune circle and the infusion sounds (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:847-857`), spawns sparks when stability is negative at an absolute-stability-in-250 chance (`:929-931`), and sparks on problem blocks while active (`:932-935`); the goggles text drawn over the tile is the extended goggles path (`client/lib/events/RenderEventHandler.java:359-381`).

### F. Stabilizers and the shield

19. **Paraphernalia count only by mirrored pairs, with diminishing returns per block type.** The scan runs a box eight blocks wide, three up and seven down from the matrix (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:708-730`), collecting pedestals and every skull or stabiliser that consents to stabilise (`:718-724`). Each candidate is paired with the position mirrored through the matrix (`:731-756`); an identical pair adds its amount scaled down by three quarters for each earlier block of the same type (`:757-765`, `:818-826`); anything less than identical — different block, different amount, or nothing on the far side, where the default amount is 0.1 — subtracts the larger of the two amounts and marks the position as a problem block (`:749-756`, `:766-769`); and a pedestal pair where only one side holds an item takes its explicit penalty instead (`:757-761` with `common/blocks/devices/BlockPedestal.java:144-162`). So a lone candle or skull costs 0.1, a matched pair gains 0.1 until the counter discounts it, and pairing a red candle with a white one is a mismatch because each dye is its own block.
20. **The amounts are 0.1 for candles and skulls, 0.025 for inlay, 0.25 for a stabilizer, and 0 or 0.1 for pedestals.** Candles (`common/blocks/basic/BlockCandle.java:87-90`), the 0.1 default for skulls and plain blocks (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:749-756`), inlay (`common/blocks/devices/BlockInlay.java:360-363`), stabilizer (`common/blocks/devices/BlockStabilizer.java:69-72`), and arcane or ancient pedestals at zero with eldritch pedestals at 0.1 (`common/blocks/devices/BlockPedestal.java:138-142`).
21. **The materials rewrite speed, cost, and replenish.** All four pillars of one type: ancient sets cycle time -1, cost -0.1, replenish -0.1; eldritch sets cycle time -3, cost +0.05, replenish +0.2 (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:775-786`); the speed and cost stones under the pillars move cycle time by -1 or +1 and cost by +0.01 or -0.02 each (`:787-799`); a pedestal's own material adds -0.01 cost when ancient and +0.0025 when eldritch (`:802-813`); and the cost multiplier floor of 0.5 is applied when a craft starts (`:326-328`). The language file states the player-level version: paraphernalia reduce instability but repeated types diminish (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1410`, `:1407`), the stones trade speed against cost (`:1416`), ancient pillars cut cost and speed slightly at an instability price while eldritch pillars cut instability but cost more, with unbalanced eldritch pedestals called out as much worse (`:1418-1424`).
22. **The stabilizer block is a battery for the shield, and the shield travels on inlay.** The tile holds at most 15 energy, regains one point per twenty ticks and pollutes a quarter unit of aura for each point regained (`common/tiles/devices/TileStabilizer.java:16-64`); its spend call succeeds only when the energy covers the request (`:101-108`); inlay and pedestals carry a charge between 0 and 15 sourced from an adjacent stabilizer's energy and decayed one step per block of distance (`common/blocks/devices/BlockInlay.java:246-275`, `:285-329`); and a pedestal's mitigation search walks the horizontal neighbours toward a source of at least five energy along the charge gradient (`common/tiles/crafting/TilePedestal.java:46-75`). When an ejection event picks a pedestal, the matrix asks that pedestal for a mitigator and, if one is found, spends 5 to 10 of its energy to cancel the entire event, flashing the shield over the pedestal with arcs to the stabilizer (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:616-628`; the shield and pedestal effects are the pedestal's client events `common/tiles/crafting/TilePedestal.java:77-96`). The same tile can also spend energy to steady flux rifts within eight blocks (`common/tiles/devices/TileStabilizer.java:66-84`), which is world scope, not altar scope. The charge rule, the fifteen-block reach, and pedestals acting as wire are described to the player at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1411`.
23. **The tally refreshes rarely, so mid-craft changes are not immediate.** The replenish value is recomputed at construction and at craft start, after any failed essentia pull (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:258-261`, `:298`, `:511`), and on the client when a caster clicks an active idle matrix (`:830-832`); during the component phase of a craft there is no rescan. Because the idle regeneration adds at least 0.1 even when the tally is negative, a finished altar never bleeds stability while idle, but the crafted value — including penalties — is added raw during a craft (`:272-279`, `:363-365`).

### G. Lifecycle, failure, and reload

24. **The order of consumption is essentia, then components, then completion.** Cycles drain essentia first (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:496-513`); once the requirement is empty they consume one component per cycle (`:514-543`); only when the component list is empty does the craft finish (`:545-546`) — the language file states this order to the player (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1407`). A component is located on any pedestal by count-insensitive equality, then held on a five-count countdown — decremented once per craft cycle, about twenty-five ticks at the baseline cadence — before the stack is replaced by its container item — an empty bucket for a water bucket — and removed from the list (`:514-531`).
25. **Stalls are survivable but costly, and starvation can run forever.** A missing component rolls a refund at probability one over its position (one for the first), adding one unit of a random still-needed aspect and subtracting 0.25 stability (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:534-541`); a failed essentia pull subtracts 0.25 and retries (`:506`); and because the craft only fails on a failed catalyst check (`:450-452`), a starved altar can run indefinitely — exactly the language file's warning that unresolved problems keep the risk coming (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1407`).
26. **Finishing hands the result into the center pedestal.** The catalyst is replaced by the result, carrying over the damage ratio for damaged tools (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:655-668`); an NBT output writes its tag under the recipe's label (`:669-675`); an enchantment output increments its level (`:676-683`); the original crafter, resolved by name, is credited with the crafting event (`:684-689`); the recipe state clears and the completion effects and sound fire (`:690-696`). Nothing else is returned: consumed components are gone, everything never required stays on its pedestal.
27. **Interruption is conclusive, not reversible.** A craft whose catalyst is removed or replaced always rolls an event per cycle (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:372-384`, `:385`) and then ends with its consumed essentia and components unrecovered, keeping only the pedestal items that remain (`:454-462`); breaking the matrix discards the in-progress state with the tile, since the shared break path only drops tile inventories and spills transport Essentia (`common/blocks/BlockTCTile.java:54-65`); breaking a pedestal drops its item (`:54-56`); and a broken structure merely deactivates the matrix (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:266-271`), which does not end a running craft unless the catalyst itself is what moved.
28. **A craft survives a reload.** The sync tag carries the two flags, the stability, the rating and the remaining requirement (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:146-162`); the save payload carries the component list, the output, the catalyst, the recipe type and the crafter's name (`:164-230`); both flows compose the same tags through the shared base (`common/tiles/TileThaumcraft.java:42-75`), so a chunk unload or a server restart restores a crafting altar and it resumes on its next cycle. Not persisted: the tick counters and the cadence, which the first update recomputes via the initial surroundings scan (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:115`, `:258-261`), and the client-only source-effect map (`:883-928`).
29. **One dead branch is worth knowing about.** Nothing in the tree ever writes a recipe type other than zero — only the constructor, the start path, and the NBT reader and writer touch it (`common/tiles/crafting/TileInfusionMatrix.java:129`, `:330`, `:182`, `:221`) — so the experience branch (which would drain a level from a player within ten blocks and hurt them each cycle, or else refund a unit of aspect into the requirement and subtract 0.25 stability) is unreachable from shipped content (`:463-492`), and its experience counter stays zero. A port should mirror the fields for save compatibility but must not treat the branch as live behavior.

## Rank-1 capture checklist

Reference setup for every step: the clean 1.12.2 profile whose only mods are the BETA26 jar and Baubles (`REF-0002` and the "Environment feasibility" section of [reference-catalog.md](reference-catalog.md)); record the profile, world seed, and game mode in a new `REF` entry before starting. The established session world is **creative**, which supplies the matrix, all pedestal and pillar variants, the stones, stabilizers, inlay, candles, skulls, and jars from the mod's creative tab without research (every Thaumcraft block sets that tab, `common/blocks/BlockTC.java:20`); a craft still needs its research key known, so grants happen with the operator command between measurements, never during one. Carry a caster, goggles (the stability readout, item 13), and jars holding the aspects a chosen recipe needs.

- **I1 — Structure and activation.** Build the altar and click the matrix with a caster: it should activate only with the center pedestal and all four pillars present; then break one pillar and record the matrix switching off within about a hundred ticks, and record the activation sound and any visual state change (items 3, 8).
- **I2 — One normal craft.** With a known recipe, place its catalyst and components and a matching essentia supply, start the craft, and record the whole run: the essentia total drawn versus the recipe's list, units visible leaving the jars, the components disappearing one at a time after a short pull delay, a bucket returning an empty bucket, and the result appearing on the center pedestal. Time the run (items 9-11, 24, 26).
- **I3 — The twelve-block draw.** Move the essentia source out to just inside and just outside twelve blocks in each axis and record whether the craft proceeds or stalls, and what a stall looks like (the goggles loss value moving, no progress) — this is the endpoint range, a box twelve below to eleven above in the source (items 11-12).
- **I4 — Instability schedule.** Run a high-rating craft (the Vis Amulet at 6) with no stabilizers and record the goggles band and loss values over time, every event that fires, whether items are dropped or destroyed, warp and potion gains, and the stability recovery after each event (items 13-17).
- **I5 — Stabilizer interception.** Repeat an ejection event with a pedestal connected by inlay to a stabilizer that has energy, and record the shield flash, the arcs, and that the item survives; then drain the stabilizer and repeat to see the item lost; record the recharge time and any aura pollution (items 17, 22).
- **I6 — Symmetry and diminishing returns.** Compare one candle, a matched pair, six candles, and a red-white mismatched pair, reading the goggles gain each time; add a lone skull and a skull pair; confirm the per-type diminishing and the mismatch penalty (items 19-20).
- **I7 — Pillars and stones.** Swap all four pillars between arcane and eldritch and re-time a fixed craft; then place one and four speed stones and one cost stone under the pillars and record the cadence and the essentia total for the same recipe (item 21).
