## Reference behavior (source-derived)

### A. The caster item's focus slot

1. **One caster item exists, and its focus is item NBT rather than a slot.** The single registration builds the caster with area zero and the name "Caster's Gauntlet" (`common/config/ConfigItems.java:275`; field `api/items/ItemsTC.java:138`; name `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:194`), it stacks to one and never takes damage (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:66-67`, `:97-99`), it is the only item implementing the caster contract (`api/casters/ICaster.java:7-21`; the only implementer, `common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:54`), and it wears the uncommon rarity (`:233-235`). The focus is carried as a full serialized item stack inside the caster's own tag compound under `focus`; readers build a fresh stack per call and the writers are exactly the swap and removal paths (`:202-231`; the only `setFocus` call sites are `common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:119`, `:158`, `:161`), so the slotted focus is not a slot in any inventory and no separate container edits it.
2. **The same tag carries the picked block and the plan-area picks.** `picked` holds a silk-touch copy of a block for a later effect to place (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:462-490`), and `areax`, `areay`, `areaz`, and `aread` hold the plan focus's area picks — the three axis picks defaulting to the area size (clamped down to it when present) and the dimension pick to zero (`common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:283-348`). Because the focus readers return fresh stacks (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:214-221`), outside code cannot mutate the stored stack through the read path.
3. **Insertion and removal run through one server-side resolver, and three client gestures send the change request.** The focus-change key (default F, "Change Caster Focus", `common/lib/events/KeyHandler.java:96-106`; registered at construction `:34-38`; constructed in `proxies/ClientProxy.java:29`) opens the radial while a caster is held in either hand (`common/lib/events/KeyHandler.java:47-62`), and while sneaking it sends the removal request instead (`common/lib/events/KeyHandler.java:56-58`); in the radial, both a hover-click and a hover-release as the ring closes with its lock clear send the hovered entry's key (`client/lib/events/WandRenderingHandler.java:289-294`, `:104-110`). Every request arrives as a single string on `PacketFocusChangeToServer` (`common/lib/network/misc/PacketFocusChangeToServer.java:26-32`), and the server performs the change against the player's own items, preferring the main hand and falling back to the off hand at packet-processing time (`:34-53`; `common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:69-167`).
4. **The kit's recipes and rarities.** The caster: an arcane craft gated `UNLOCKAUROMANCY@2`, a hundred vis and one of each primal aspect (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:193`). The pouch: `FOCUSPOUCH`, twenty-five vis, a mundane belt with leather and gold (`:233`). The blank lesser focus: crucible-crafted under `UNLOCKAUROMANCY` with an order crystal as catalyst (`:146`); the advanced and greater blanks: infusions that upgrade the lesser and advanced blanks (`:278-279`). Those five recipes are the only ones in the tree that mention the caster, the blanks, or the pouch, so day-to-day foci move between players as ordinary items. Rarities: caster and pouch uncommon (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:233-235`; `common/items/casters/ItemFocusPouch.java:32-34`), every focus rare (`common/items/casters/ItemFocus.java:153-155`).

### B. The Focus Pouch

5. **The pouch is one item: an eighteen-slot, focus-only inventory stored in its own tag and worn on the belt.** One variant, stacking to one, no damage (`common/items/casters/ItemFocusPouch.java:21-26`); the contents are read and written from the item tag as a list of eighteen entries (`:47-60`); it is a belt bauble (`:62-64`); uncommon rarity (`:32-34`); right-clicking opens GUI 5 on the server side (`:40-45`; screen and container mappings `proxies/ProxyGUI.java:95-97`, `:158-160`).
6. **The screen is a standard container over a temporary eighteen-slot inventory.** The input inventory refuses anything that is not a focus and limits each slot to one (`common/container/InventoryFocusPouch.java:8-22`; slot guard `common/container/slot/SlotLimitedByClass.java:27-29`), and the container lays it out as a six-by-three grid above the player inventory (`common/container/ContainerFocusPouch.java:39-41`, `:59-68`). The pouch's tag is loaded into the slots on the server only (`:43-51`), and on close the slots are written back into the pouch tag and the held stack is refreshed (`:108-126`); the slot the pouch was opened from is locked against interaction (`:37-38`, `:70-73`, `:101-106`). The client screen draws the opening slot highlighted, never hotbar-swaps, and closes itself as soon as the hotbar slot it was opened from is empty (`client/gui/GuiFocusPouch.java:20-26`, `:34-42`, `:44-46`, `:48-51`; size `:24-25`).
7. **Manual use is drag-and-drop between the pouch and the backpack.** The screen is a normal inventory screen, so foci move both ways by hand or shift-click, guarded by the same focus-only test (`common/container/ContainerFocusPouch.java:79-86`); nothing else reads or writes the pouch's contents while it is open.
8. **The swap path prefers pouches, and the research text describes it.** Returning a focus first tries every carried pouch, then the backpack (`common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:117-123`, `:157-165`; placement `:191-221`); the pouches are enumerated from the belt bauble slots first, then from the backpack (`:76-116`); retrieving a focus removes it from the pouch tag and writes the pouch back in place (`:169-189`); and the player-facing statement of the behavior is in the research page ("it will check this pouch first", `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1645-1646`).
9. **What persists, and what the client needs to see it.** The contents are item tag, so they travel with the stack through drops, chests, handoffs, and world saves; the radial reads pouch contents from the local client's own copies of the pouch items (`client/lib/events/WandRenderingHandler.java:153-174`, `:187-202`), which is what the pouch declares for itself with a share flag (`common/items/casters/ItemFocusPouch.java:28-30`; the hand mirror is the only other item in the tree that declares it, `common/items/tools/ItemHandMirror.java:41`) and the same flag the mod's own stack serializer consults when deciding whether NBT rides a serialized stack (`common/lib/utils/Utils.java:494-498`).

### C. The Vis-discount economy

10. **The discount is an interface, summed over baubles then armour, and dragged negative by the exhaustion potions.** `api/items/IVisDiscountGear.java:11-17` (a percentage, with a documented fifty-percent ceiling and per-item guidance of five percent); the sum walks the bauble inventory first, then the four armour slots, then subtracts ten percent per level of the stronger vis-exhaustion potion (`common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:26-54`). Nothing in the read path clamps the sum.
11. **The items that feed it, with their player-facing names.** Goggles of Revealing, five (`common/items/armor/ItemGoggles.java:77-79`; name `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:108`). Thaumaturge's Robe and Leggings, three each, and Boots, two (`common/items/armor/ItemRobeArmor.java:115-117`; names `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:119-121`). Void Thaumaturge Hood, Robe, and Leggings, five each (`common/items/armor/ItemVoidRobeArmor.java:112-114`; names `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:126-128`). Crimson Cult Robe pieces, one each, and Crimson Cult Boots, one (`common/items/armor/ItemCultistRobeArmor.java:73-75`; `common/items/armor/ItemCultistBoots.java:69-71`; names `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:129-132`). The Apprentice's Ring, five — the only one of the seven mundane and fancy bauble variants that carries a discount at all (`common/items/baubles/ItemBaubles.java:14`, `:43-48`; name `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:175`). The Voidseer's Pearl, scaling with permanent warp up to a quarter (`common/items/baubles/ItemVoidseerCharm.java:44-52`; name `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:186`). Worn together these can pass the interface's documented fifty-percent ceiling, since goggles, the three void pieces, and the ring alone sum to twenty-five and the pearl's quarter is additive.
12. **The discount becomes a consumption modifier with a tenth floor.** One minus the summed fraction, never lower than a tenth (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:193-200`), so even a sum far past the documented ceiling stops at ninety percent off; the crafting flag is accepted and ignored. This is the only arithmetic that stands between the discount list and the payment (the payment itself is cas-02's item 3).
13. **The player-facing surfaces disagree by design.** The caster's tooltip prints the focus's raw, undiscounted vis figure (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:237-254`; the figure `common/items/casters/ItemFocus.java:157-160`; keys `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:198`, `:1056`), and the discount appears only on the gear itself, as a per-item tooltip line (`common/lib/events/PlayerEvents.java:191-198`, `:302-308`; key `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1055`) — so the actual discounted drain never appears on the item tooltips; it is printed only by the caster's own dial, as a numeric line while its holder sneaks (item 21, `client/lib/events/HudHandler.java:391-403`) — and a caster with any tag shows the cost line even when nothing is slotted (blank text, `common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:239-248`).

### D. Cooldowns

14. **Two static maps, one per side, keyed by entity id, booked before any payment decision.** `common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:23-24` (declared), `:377-380` (initialized); the cast path books an entry on every click that passes the focus and cooldown test, before any resource test (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:382-385`, `:397`; the booking order is also cas-02's item 4); the value is the activation time of the slotted focus (`common/items/casters/ItemFocus.java:162-165`; the activation arithmetic was derived in cas-02's item 3). One entry per entity, so every caster and both hands on one player share a single window (`common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:350-355`, `:364-375`).
15. **Expiry is wall-clock time, and each side reads only its own book.** Entries are stamped as the current wall time plus the activation time in ticks times fifty milliseconds (`:370`, `:373`), and the check compares against the current wall time (`:351-354`); the client tests and writes only the client map and the server only the server map, and nothing transmits either value — the client side of the same click books its window independently of the server's (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:382-402`).
16. **The window length comes from each side's own copy of the focus.** Both sides compute the activation time from the package tag of the stack they hold (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:385`), so if the two copies ever differ — a stale pouch or focus copy client-side — the two windows differ in both length and start time, and the drift compounds per swap.
17. **Two pieces of the cooldown code are unreachable in play.** The remaining-seconds reader is client-only and has no callers anywhere in the tree (`common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:357-362`; the boolean check's only consumer is the cast path, `:350-355` from `common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:382`), so BETA26 shows no numeric cooldown readout; and the clear-both-maps branch (`common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:364-368`) is reachable only with an activation time of zero, which the five-tick floor prevents for any packaged focus (`common/items/casters/ItemFocus.java:162-165`) — only the package-less edge in item 27 would return zero.

### E. The caster's render surface

18. **Item-side surfaces: model property, tint, equip behaviour, pose.** A client-only model property flips when a focus is inserted (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:68-77`); the caster's tint comes from the slotted focus's lazily cached colour (`client/ColorHandler.java:190-195`; the cache `common/items/casters/ItemFocus.java:38-70`), and the focus items tint fully from the same colour (`client/ColorHandler.java:196-203`); the hotbar re-equip animation is skipped when two caster stacks' slotted foci hash the same (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:80-95`); the item renders in 3D and its use action is the bow pose over a nominal hour-long use window with no start or stop handling, so the pose is cosmetic (`:101-104`, `:405-411`).
19. **Tooltips.** The caster's tooltip shows the vis cost line, the slotted focus's display name, and the focus's own node listing (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:237-254`); the focus tooltip prints the vis-per-cast figure and walks the node tree, indenting beneath splits and tagging each setting with its value (`common/items/casters/ItemFocus.java:102-151`; names and keys `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:195-198`).
20. **The radial HUD is the caster's main screen.** It renders inside the overlay pass (`client/lib/events/RenderEventHandler.java:286-289`) as two counter-rotating rings sized by the number of carried foci (`client/lib/events/WandRenderingHandler.java:237-257`), with the slotted focus at the centre and its tooltip (`:258-266`) and the carried foci around it (`:269-301`); opening it releases the mouse and the fade-back re-grabs it (`:78-102`, `:123-137`); hovering enlarges an entry and a click sends its key once, guarded by a lock (`:283-295`); it does not open at all with no foci to show (`:88-94`). The entries are item 3's map built fresh from the client's own inventory copies (`:147-204`), and the keybinds are declared in `common/lib/events/KeyHandler.java:96-106`.
21. **The aura readout on the caster's own dial is fed by the caster's own push.** While a caster stack ticks in its holder's inventory, it sends its pool's vis, flux, and base to its holder's client every ten ticks (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:260-269`, `:271-319`; payload `common/lib/network/misc/PacketAuraToClient.java:21-37`; registration `common/lib/network/PacketHandler.java:65`). The surface that shows it is the caster's own HUD: whenever a caster is held in either hand (`client/lib/events/HudHandler.java:89-110`, definition `:345`), the dial draws the aura bar from that pushed value (`:372-389`) and, while the holder sneaks, the numeric vis plus the focus's actual discounted cast figure (`:391-403`). The thaumometer's HUD shows the same pushed value while a thaumometer is held, because the thaumometer pushes it as well (`common/items/tools/ItemThaumometer.java:99`; the HUD's thaumometer branch `client/lib/events/HudHandler.java:98-102`). This is the only server-to-client caster packet besides effect FX (cas-02's item 22; visuals are `CLI-02`).
22. **The plan focus's world overlay is the caster's other surface.** The caster implements the architect contract and delegates to the first node that does (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:413-460`), and the overlay and axis arrows draw from the drawn handler (`client/lib/events/WandRenderingHandler.java:314-336`, `:352-452`) on two live paths — every render tick (`client/lib/events/RenderEventHandler.java:216-231`) and the world-last pass (`:438-445`) — plus a highlight-gated path the caster itself disables (`:411-419`; `common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:457-460`). The overlay's gate is the plan package check (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:445-455`), and the area and axis it uses are the caster's own tags (item 2) switched by the key path in item 23.

### F. Multiplayer, and the divergence classes

23. **Three packets carry the caster's multiplayer traffic, and one keyboard gesture writes the area tags.** Registration: the two server-bound messages at `common/lib/network/PacketHandler.java:73-74` and the aura message at `:65` (item 21). The focus-change request carries one string — a focus key or the removal token (`common/lib/network/misc/PacketFocusChangeToServer.java:26-32`); the item-key request carries a key byte and a modifier byte (`common/lib/network/misc/PacketItemKeyToServer.java:33-41`), and its handler routes key one to the caster toggle in the main hand, then the off hand, else to the elemental shovel (`:43-73`). The modifier comes from the keyboard: plain selects the position cycle, control the axis cycle, shift no branch at all (`common/lib/events/KeyHandler.java:82` vs `common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:231-270`), and the toggle applies only when the slotted focus's graph contains a plan node (`:227-235`), writing the area tags only while the caster already has a tag (`:326-347`).
24. **The client proposes, the server disposes — with a fuzzy resolver.** The server resolves the requested key against its own map: an exact key wins; otherwise the next key above it; otherwise the first key (`common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:124-131`); and when the server's map of carried foci is empty, every request behaves as a removal, unslotting the held focus into a pouch or the backpack (`:117-123`). A stale or unrecognised key therefore selects a different focus than the one clicked, or unslots the held one — the divergence class the acceptance line names, invisible to the clicking player until the result lands.
25. **What is never sent.** No packet carries caster, focus, or pouch NBT; every client-side focus read — tooltips, the tint, the radial list, pouch contents (items 18-20) — runs on the client's own copies of the stacks, and the server's resolver (item 24) runs on its own. The only caster-related syncs are the aura push (item 21) and effect FX (cas-02's item 22). Nearby players therefore see another player's caster only as an ordinary held item, tinted from that stack's synced NBT.
26. **The client always answers its own click, and the server's refusals are silent.** The client returns success for any eligible click before payment and never casts (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:394-396`); the server refuses silently when the resources are short — no cast, no swing — while the activation delay stays booked (`:397-402`, `:382-385`; the refusal inventory is cas-02's item 32). The only gate the client applies to a repeated click is its own cooldown book (items 14-16).
27. **Edges with crash or data shape.** (a) A focus stack that carries a tag but no package (an anvil rename adds a display tag to an otherwise blank focus) is dereferenced without a guard by the focus-list builders — the radial's pouch and inventory loops and the server's swap loop (`common/items/casters/ItemFocus.java:72-82`; `client/lib/events/WandRenderingHandler.java:163-170`, `:177-186`; `common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:84-90`, `:95-101`). (b) The pouch screen binds its pouch from the main-hand item and keep-open-tests the main-hand hotbar slot (`common/container/ContainerFocusPouch.java:37-38`; `client/gui/GuiFocusPouch.java:23`, `:48-51`), so an off-hand open can bind the wrong stack and/or close immediately. (c) The readers trust the stored stack: the focus getter casts the stored item to a focus without a guard and the removal path assumes a tag exists (`common/items/casters/ItemCaster.java:202-212`, `:224-231`) — both unreachable through the UI's own writers, so record them only if a session reaches them.
28. **Dormant plumbing a port must not copy as live behaviour.** The plan area size is computed from a flag that is permanently false, so the size is always three and the larger value never occurs (`common/items/casters/CasterManager.java:273-281`); the key packet's shift modifier reaches no handler branch (item 23). The sibling dormant set on the execution side is cas-02's item 37.

## Rank-1 capture checklist

Reference setup for every step: the clean 1.12.2 profile whose only mods are the BETA26 jar and Baubles (`REF-0002` and the "Environment feasibility" section of [reference-catalog.md](reference-catalog.md)); record the profile, world seed, and game mode in a new `REF` entry before starting. The session world is creative, which supplies the caster, the three blank foci, the Focus Pouch, and the discount gear from the mod's creative tab without research; grant node patterns with the operator command between measurements, never during one (`/thaumcraft research <player> <key>`, with its list, all, reset, and revoke forms — `common/lib/CommandThaumcraft.java:72-80`, `:89-105`). Carry a stack of aspect crystals for any imprints; the caster's own dial shows the aura readout whenever it is held (`client/lib/events/HudHandler.java:91-92`), and while sneaking it prints the numeric vis and the discounted cast figure (item 21).

- **T1 — The radial, slotting, and the item surfaces.** With `BASEAUROMANCY` granted, imprint a root-touch-fire focus in the manipulator (sibling checklist), hold the caster, and press F: record the mouse release, the ring listing the focus, its hover tooltip, and a selection — by click, or by releasing the ring key with an entry hovered; then record the caster's tooltip reading "Vis cost:" with the focus's figure (0.8 for this focus — cas-01's item 25) followed by the focus's node listing, the caster model's focus tint, and that pressing F with no foci anywhere does not open the ring (items 3, 18-20).
- **T2 — Swap, removal, and data fidelity.** With a second imprinted focus in the backpack, swap via the ring and compare both tooltips before and after — nodes, settings, and cast cost must read identically; then sneak+F and record the removal, the two different tick-sound pitches, and where the removed focus lands (pouch first when one is carried, backpack otherwise — items 3, 8).
- **T3 — The pouch at hand.** Right-click the pouch: record the eighteen focus-only slots, the refusal of a non-focus item, the opening-slot highlight, and that the screen closes when the held pouch is swapped away; put two foci in, close and reopen, then exit and reload the world — the contents must persist; move one focus out by hand and back (items 5-7).
- **T4 — The pouch at the belt and in the ring.** Belt-wear the pouch with foci inside, press F, and record that belt-pouch foci are listed and that selecting one pulls it from the pouch while the removed focus goes back in (item 8; the research text's own description, `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1646`); then fill the pouch completely and swap again from a backpack focus: the removed focus must land in the backpack.
- **T5 — Cooldown and refusal feel.** Click once and click again immediately: record that nothing happens and measure the window against the focus's activation time (a small focus floors at a quarter second; items 14-16). Drain the local aura by repeated casting, then record an insufficient-vis cast — no effect, no swing, nothing visible — and that the next click is still blocked for the window (items 14, 26; cas-02's item 32); if clicking fast enough at the boundary, record whether a second cast slips through or is swallowed (item 15).
- **T6 — Multiplayer sweep** (LAN with a second client if available; otherwise record as not-run, the same gap as `FND-01`'s dedicated-server procedure). Have player B swap and remove foci and cast while A watches: record what A sees of B's caster (the model tint from B's synced NBT, and nothing else — item 25), that B's own tooltip matches the server result, and both aura readouts after a cast (item 21); record whether A can ever read B's slotted focus cost (item 25).
- **T7 — The edge arsenal.** (a) Rename a blank focus in an anvil, keep it in the backpack, and press F: record a crash or not (item 27a).
