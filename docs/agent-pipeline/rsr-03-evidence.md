## Target behaviors to capture

## Reference behavior (source-derived)

### A. The Thaumonomicon item and the book it opens

1. **One item with two damages; the cheat variant is off by default.** `common/items/curios/ItemThaumonomicon.java:34-40` registers the item with the "normal" and "cheat" variants, subtypes on, stack size one; the cheat variant appears in the creative list only when the misc config enables the cheat sheet (`:43-50`; the switch defaults to false at `common/config/ModConfig.java:410-415` and `:426-429`) and its tooltip marks it as creative-only (`:52-58`). Rarity is uncommon, or epic for the cheat variant (`:95-97`). The player-facing names are the Thaumonomicon and the Cheater's Thaumonomicon (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:7-8`).
2. **Right-click opens the book, and the cheat variant grants everything on the server.** `common/items/curios/ItemThaumonomicon.java:60-93`: the server branch of the cheat variant walks every category and every entry and grants each recursively (`:61-70`); otherwise it walks every entry the player already knows and completes its not-yet-known siblings (`:71-85`) — this is the sibling mechanic of the research data, e.g. `FIRSTSTEPS` declares the siblings `KNOWLEDGETYPES` and `!gotdream` (`assets/thaumcraft/research/basics.json:4-27`) — then syncs knowledge to the player (`common/items/curios/ItemThaumonomicon.java:86`). The client branch plays the page sound (`:88-90`) and both sides open GUI id 12 (`:91`), which the proxy maps to the browser screen (`proxies/ProxyGUI.java:68-70`). The book is a plain client screen over the synced knowledge capability, not a container; its only server writes are the two progression/flag packets (both covered in [rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md), items 17-18).
3. **Entry preconditions are pseudo-keys granted outside the book.** Picking the item up grants `!gotthaumonomicon` (`common/lib/events/PlayerEvents.java:145-148`), and `!gotdream` is granted by sleeping (or on a crystal pickup when the no-sleep config is set) and is the dust-trigger key that turns a bookshelf into the Thaumonomicon (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:71-72`; `common/lib/events/PlayerEvents.java:152-164`). So the first `FIRSTSTEPS` parent is satisfied by possession of the item, not by any book interaction.

### B. The book shell: categories, entries, and how progress is shown

4. **The browser is a pan-and-zoom map of one category at a time.** `client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:100-161` are the two constructors (the no-argument one reads the saved map-position statics; the coordinate pair serves the return-from-page path); `:162-166` builds the category buttons; `:392-491` is the render loop, with wheel zoom clamped between 1 and 2 (`:435`), click-drag panning clamped to the computed content bounds, and a remembered position that survives closing the book (`:294-299`). Each category supplies its own background(s) drawn beneath the icons (`:519-537`).
5. **Category columns and their gates.** The left column lists the seven mod categories in the order the static list defines (`common/config/ConfigResearch.java:333-336`), the right column the addon categories, with scroll buttons when the right column overflows (`client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:218-244`). A category appears only when its own research key is strictly known, so e.g. Auromancy is invisible until `UNLOCKAUROMANCY` is complete (`:213-215`; category keys registered at `common/config/ConfigResearch.java:137-145`). Each button draws its icon and, on hover, the localized name plus a completion percentage (`client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:1070-1160`). The percentage counts the category's entries excluding `AUTOUNLOCK` ones, and counts an entry as known through the loose knowledge test (`:200-217`) — the same two knowledge tests the scan lane uses ([rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md), item 12).
6. **Entry visibility is prerequisite-driven, with a session-local hidden list.** `client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:268-292`: a known entry is always visible; an unknown entry with the `HIDDEN` meta is visible only once its requisites pass; an entry whose parent is not visible is hidden and its key is recorded in the per-session hidden list; the requisite test is the server-side predicate read through the client capability (`ResearchManager.doesPlayerHaveRequisites`).
7. **Icon state encodes progression at a glance.** Complete entries draw in full color; an unlockable unknown entry pulses (a sine over a 600 ms clock, `:605-612`); a locked entry draws darkened with a black-and-white icon (`:613-620`, `:660-663`). Meta shapes/backgrounds distinguish rounded, spiky, hex, and hidden entries (`:622-650`). A `RESEARCH` flag draws the "newly discovered" badge, a `PAGE` flag the "new page added" badge (`:644-659`), and any entry with a stage that carries warp more than zero gets the forbidden glyph beside the icon (`:587-604`, `:1013-1020`).
8. **Connections are drawn per parent and sibling pair, colored by knowledge.** `client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:539-580`: parent links run grey while known and near-black while unknown, sibling links in blue tones, skipping the pairs that a sibling listing already implies and honouring the `REVERSE` meta.
9. **The hover tooltip is the progression readout.** `client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:761-796`: name; for an unlockable entry either "current stage n/m" or "not yet begun"; for a locked one the "missing required research" line plus the localized names of the unknown parents; then the new-research and new-page lines when those flags are set (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1053`, `:1073-1076`).
10. **Clicking an entry either starts it or opens it.** `client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:803-822`: unknown-and-unlockable sends the progress request packet, opens the page, and shows a short popup naming the entry; known opens the page directly and first clears the two display flags both locally and on the server. A third branch nudges a fully-read entry's stage bookkeeping (`:816-819`). Every one of these routes is the client proposing and the server disposing, per [rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md), item 17.
11. **Search reaches categories, entries, and the recipe catalog.** `client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:327-390`: category names, known-entry names, and the outputs of the recipes listed on the entry's *next* stage (stage plus one, clamped to the last stage, `:355`), each recipe resolved through the catalog and the vanilla registry, deduplicated per draw; the results list categories, entries, and recipes with click-through to their screens (`:823-855`), and the field is capped at 15 characters (`:173-179`; strings at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1071-1072`).
12. **The page is a two-page spread driven by the stored stage.** `client/gui/GuiResearchPage.java:155-225` builds the screen, converts the player's known-aspect pseudo-keys into the aspect insert list (`:213-220`), and parses the pages; `:1730-1930` maps the entry's stored stage to the spread and appends every addendum whose research is strictly known (`:1755-1768`), with `<BR>`, `<LINE>`, `<DIV>`, `<PAGE>` and `<IMG>` markup handled by that parser. A re-parse runs every two seconds, or every 250 ms while a completion click is being held (`:231-245`); page navigation is by keyboard (arrow keys, page keys) and by the on-screen corner arrows (`:1470-1495`, `:1498-1524`, `:1612-1624`), with page sounds.
13. **A stage is completed from the page's requirement rows.** `client/gui/GuiResearchPage.java:499-737` draws, top to bottom, the research row (icons for research keys, aspect icons for `!` aspect forms, placeholder icons for the `m_`, `c_`, and `f_` pseudo-key families, `:503-587`), the obtain row (`:588-618`), the craft row keyed on the `[#]`-hashed craft references with a tick per satisfied entry (`:619-649`), and the knowledge row with type icon, amount, and a tick when the player's pools cover it (`:650-707`). When any row exists and every requisite is satisfied, a 64×12 complete button is drawn; clicking it sends the progress request with the cost-checking option enabled and plays the write sound, and while the click is pending the button reads as completing (`:708-736`, `:1527-1536`; strings at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1082-1084`, `:1095-1096`). The server-side cost deduction and validation are [rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md), item 17.
14. **Warp and the two insert panels are page furniture.** A stage with warp more than zero shows the forbidden glyph and the warp-level name (clamped at five) plus a warned popup (`client/gui/GuiResearchPage.java:403-412`; level names at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1043-1047`, warning at `:1097`). The aspect insert unlocks once `FIRSTSTEPS` is complete and the knowledge insert once `KNOWLEDGETYPES` is complete — the buttons appear beside the page and toggle a paper overlay (`client/gui/GuiResearchPage.java:365-398`, `:416-430`, `:1059-1070`); the knowledge panel lists per type and category totals with the partial remainder (`:432-497`; type names at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1157-1158`).
15. **Recipes are bookmarked on the right edge of the page and opened as recipe spreads.** `client/gui/GuiResearchPage.java:739-784` draws one bookmark per recipe key of the current stage, cycling the output icon; `:786-906` resolves each key through the crafting catalog, the fake catalog, the vanilla registry, and the compiled recipe groups, and admits only research-carrying recipes through a strict-known test — crucible `:845-852`, infusion `:853-886`, arcane `:887-894` — while plain vanilla crafting recipes are admitted without a research test (`:895-899`). Clicking a bookmark shows the typed recipe spread: arcane `:1174-1239`, crafting `:1241-1286`, crucible `:1288-1343`, infusion `:1345-1468`, and the multiblock blueprint `:960-979`; a back stack returns to the previous recipe (`:1516-1524`, `:1641-1658`).
16. **Ingredient icons inside a page link to their recipe when the player may know it.** `client/gui/GuiResearchPage.java:1687-1712` with `:2008-2056` and `:2058-2090`: the hovered stack's recipe key is searched across every stage's recipe lists; a recipe whose research is unknown reports the "cannot craft yet" state, otherwise the tooltip gains the click-for-recipe line and the icon becomes a link (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:783-785`).

### C. The research table block, tile, and theory state

17. **The block opens GUI 10 and is furniture otherwise.** `common/blocks/crafting/BlockResearchTable.java:26-48` (wooden, non-full cube, drops itself), `:50-56` opens the container on the server and returns early on the client, `:58-63` faces the placer, and `:65-82` spawns drifting particle motes above the table only while a theory is in progress. Its two player-facing strings are the Research Table name and the no-ink / no-paper prompts (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:313`, `:857-859`).
18. **The table is made from a placed wooden table, not from a recipe.** `common/blocks/basic/BlockTable.java:50-67`: right-clicking the wooden table variant while holding an item that implements the scribe-tools interface swaps the block for the research-table block, seeds slot 0 with a copy of the tools, clears the held stack, and fires the vanilla player-crafting event with the research-table stack. That event is what satisfies the `thaumcraft:research_table` craft requirement of the `THEORYRESEARCH` stage, whose stage 1 also requires the scribing tools and lists the table and the inkwell as its recipe bookmarks (`assets/thaumcraft/research/basics.json:38-54`). The table and scribing tools recipes themselves are ordinary recipes registered outside research (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:461-466`), and the tools are a single unstackable item with a 100-point ink bar (`common/items/tools/ItemScribingTools.java:6-13`; interface `api/items/IScribeTools.java:1-12`).
19. **The tile is a two-slot inventory: scribing tools and paper.** `common/tiles/crafting/TileResearchTable.java:39-47` (two slots, both synced, data field); `:167-184` accepts only a scribe-tools item in slot 0 and blank paper in slot 1; `:142-160` implement ink and paper consumption, each bumping the tile sync; the client update packet triggers a client-side block update notification (`:186-192`). Breaking the block returns the stacks through the base inventory behavior; the container itself is `common/container/ContainerResearchTable.java`.
20. **A theory's whole state lives on the tile and rides its sync NBT under one note tag.** `common/tiles/crafting/TileResearchTable.java:49-69`: the read path deserializes a `ResearchTableData` from the note tag and leaves the data null when absent; the write path serializes it back. The theory object (`api/research/theorycraft/ResearchTableData.java:18-37`) carries the owner name, inspiration and its starting value, bonus draws, the two vestigial counters, penalty start, saved card seeds, the aid card list, the per-category totals map, the blocked-category list, and the current card choices. Full serialization is `:95-165`, deserialization `:167-233` — the client renders the server's note, so both sides see the same cards and totals.
21. **Starting a theory is a server-side action from one packet.** The payload is the table position and the chosen aid keys (`common/lib/network/misc/PacketStartTheoryToServer.java:17-53`); the handler runs on the server thread, resolves the tile, and calls `TileResearchTable.startNewTheory` (`common/tiles/crafting/TileResearchTable.java:78-82`), which creates the data object and syncs. This packet and the completion flow below are the table's only server-bound traffic.
22. **Inspiration is knowledge-derived, and each chosen aid costs one point.** `api/research/theorycraft/ResearchTableData.java:326-338`: the start value comes from the player's knowledge — five points plus half a point per known entry with the `SPIKY` meta and a tenth per known `HIDDEN` entry, rounded and capped at fifteen (`:353-367`) — and each selected aid reduces the starting inspiration by one. `common/tiles/crafting/TileResearchTable.java:100-140` scans a 9×9×3 block box around the table for aid blocks and items and every entity within five blocks for aid entity classes, returning the aid keys the GUI offers (`:100-140`); the interface documents the box and the one-shot card rule (`api/research/theorycraft/ITheorycraftAid.java:10-28`).
23. **Aid registrations cover the seven categories plus curios and landmarks.** `common/config/ConfigResearch.java:227-275` registers the aid set — a bookshelf, a brain in a jar, a glyphed stone, three portal variants, one for each of the six non-basics categories, an enchantment table, and a beacon — alongside the standard card pool. A bookshelf alone contributes one universal-theory card, two notation cards, and three study cards (`api/research/theorycraft/AidBookshelf.java:5-17`); a category aid contributes three themed cards (`common/lib/research/theorycraft/AidBasicAlchemy.java:7-18`).
24. **Cards are registered classes with a small contract.** `api/research/theorycraft/TheorycraftManager.java:9-25` holds the aid and card registries keyed by class name; `api/research/theorycraft/TheorycraftCard.java:15-137` defines a seeded card with an inspiration cost (which may be zero or negative), an optional research category, required items with per-item consumption flags, an initialization hook that can veto the card, and an activation hook that performs the effect. The base cards live in the API package (study, analyze, balance, notation, ponder, rethink, reject, experimentation, inspired) and the themed pool in `common/lib/research/theorycraft`. Two examples: the study card grants 15-25 progress in a randomly chosen available category at cost one (`api/research/theorycraft/CardStudy.java:35-40`, `:48-50`, `:63-66`), the analyze card is present in the registry but dormant as shipped — its initializer can never arm (section E, item 38) — and is written to spend a stored observation point converting it into category progress, also seeding the fundamentals category (`api/research/theorycraft/CardAnalyze.java:39-56`, `:71-79`; registration `common/config/ConfigResearch.java:243`).
25. **Drawing is server-side, probabilistic, and bounded.** `api/research/theorycraft/ResearchTableData.java:239-293`: bonus draws are consumed first and a draw without one yields two cards, each drawn card must fit the remaining inspiration and an available category, duplicates within one draw are rejected, an aid card has a one-in-four chance to be drawn while aid cards remain and is then removed from the aid list permanently, and a failsafe caps the loop. Category availability is the strictly-known category key, minus the blocked list (`:235-237`, `:341-351`). The card draw costs one sheet of paper from the table (`common/container/ContainerResearchTable.java:113-119`, `common/tiles/crafting/TileResearchTable.java:152-160`).
26. **Picking a card is server-side too, with an anti-spam window and item handling.** `common/container/ContainerResearchTable.java:65-102`: the three card buttons are rate-limited per player by a 333 ms window (`:67-74`), required items are checked against the player's inventory with an ore-aware carrying test, the flagged subset is consumed, the card's activation hook runs, the used ink is deducted, the choice is marked selected, and its inspiration cost is subtracted. The activation result gates the whole sequence: a card that reports failure spends nothing. The commit step — sent automatically by the client's selection handshake (`client/gui/GuiResearchTable.java:738`) — banks the outgoing current card, appending its seed to the saved list, and then promotes the selected choice to current (`:50-64`).
27. **Paper is a hard server gate; ink is a client gate.** Drawing requires the paper stack (`:113-119`), but for card activation the ink deduction's result is discarded (`:91-97`) — the enforced guard is the client screen, which refuses the click when the tools are absent or used up (`client/gui/GuiResearchTable.java:708-713`) and disables the create button in the same conditions (`:638-664`). This is the table's version of the client-led-progress class `RSR-01` names, and it is only separable in multiplayer (checklist T6).
28. **The table screen lays the loop out and exposes the numbers.** `client/gui/GuiResearchTable.java:83-140` (buttons: create theory, complete theory, scrap theory), `:130-330` (render: aid chooser with its inspiration bulbs before a theory starts, `:200-262`; the sheet stack, the merged current card and saved cards, `:263-312`; per-category totals as percentages with the penalty markers and gain sparkles, `:313-395`), `:601-612` (button placement), `:614-636` (actions: create sends the start packet; complete and scrap go through the container's button channel), `:666-723` (hit areas: aid toggles, card click, paper-stack click that draws), `:725-745` (selection handshake), `:747-757` (the four UI sounds). The card faces are drawn from their serialized seeds (`:455-565`, `:567-583`), so the same card data renders on both clients.

### D. Theorycrafting's payoff, and research-driven recipe discovery

29. **Completing a theory is a conversion, not a completion of an entry.** `common/tiles/crafting/TileResearchTable.java:84-98`: the category totals are sorted descending, each category's total is converted to theory knowledge proportional to the knowledge type's progression constant with a two-thirds penalty for every category past the penalty start — a fresh theory starts at zero, so only the top category is unpenalized, and five cards raise the start by one each: balance, beacon, celestial, revelation, and synergy (`api/research/theorycraft/CardBalance.java:50`, `common/lib/research/theorycraft/CardBeacon.java:33`, `common/lib/research/theorycraft/CardCelestial.java:111`, `common/lib/research/theorycraft/CardRevelation.java:41`, `common/lib/research/theorycraft/CardSynergy.java:61`) — and a floor of one point, and the results are granted through the internal knowledge handler with a category field each. The data object's completion condition is simply that inspiration ran out (`api/research/theorycraft/ResearchTableData.java:69-71`), and completing or scrapping clears the theory (`common/tiles/crafting/TileResearchTable.java:84-98` clears the data; the container's buttons gate on the same condition, `common/container/ContainerResearchTable.java:103-112`). The knowledge constants, the sync packet, and the learn-sound feedback belong to [rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md), items 14-15.
30. **The conversion is what pays the book's knowledge rows.** Stage knowledge requirements are paid from those pools when the page's complete button runs the server-side cost check ([rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md), item 17); the page's own knowledge row displays exactly those pooled amounts (`client/gui/GuiResearchPage.java:650-707`). So the loop a port must reproduce is: scan and theorycraft feed pools, pools pay stage requirements, stages hand out recipes and unlock further categories.
31. **Knowledge and recipe gating flow through one catalog keyed by resource location.** `api/internal/CommonInternals.java:24-37` holds a crafting catalog and a fake catalog (display-only entries such as multiblock blueprints) with their accessors; `api/ThaumcraftApi.java:85-90` exposes both, `:108-113` adds blueprints, and `:219-221` adds crucible recipes; `common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:560-608` compiles both catalogs into recipe groups so one bookmark can cover several recipe keys. The research JSON names the catalog keys directly — e.g. `thaumcraft:thaumometer`, `thaumcraft:tablewood`, `thaumcraft:inkwell` (`assets/thaumcraft/research/basics.json:15-25`, `:48-52`).
32. **The crafting-time gate is each recipe's research field, checked against the crafter.** Crucible recipes: the matcher requires the recipe's research to be strictly known before a matching recipe is considered and then picks the recipe with the highest aspect cost (`common/lib/crafting/ThaumcraftCraftingManager.java:54-74`, called from `common/tiles/crafting/TileCrucible.java:178`). Infusion recipes: the research test lives inside the recipe's own match (`api/crafting/InfusionRecipe.java:55-65`, getter `:67-69`). Arcane recipes declare the key in the interface (`api/crafting/IArcaneRecipe.java:1-11`) and are gated in the workbench result path ([rsr-02-evidence.md](rsr-02-evidence.md), item 7). The Thaumatorium's recipe selection and the focal manipulator's node gates use the same strict test (`common/tiles/crafting/TileThaumatorium.java:451`, `common/tiles/crafting/TileFocalManipulator.java:248`). The strict knowledge test honours the `@stage` suffix, and the recipe lookups that ignore research (the arcane registry walk) are covered by [rsr-02-evidence.md](rsr-02-evidence.md), item 12.
33. **The data-side surface a port must load matches the discovery surface.** The research tree is eight registered JSON files plus a reference example under one data root (`assets/thaumcraft/research/`: a basics file of twenty entries plus alchemy, artifice, auromancy, eldritch, golemancy, infusion, and the scan-only file, with `_example.json.txt` beside them), loaded through the registered research locations (`common/config/ConfigResearch.java:122-131`) and parsed at post-init (`:133-135`); categories and their required research keys are declared in code (`:137-145`). The JSON schema for an entry is stage-based with `text`, `recipes`, `required_craft`, `required_knowledge`, `required_research`, and `obtain` forms as shown by the first-steps entry (`assets/thaumcraft/research/basics.json:4-27`) and the reference example shipped beside the API (`api/research/_example.json.txt`). The knowledge-structure semantics of those stage forms belong to [rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md).

### E. Dormant paths and artifacts a port must not copy

34. **The start-research popup has no translation.** `client/gui/GuiResearchBrowser.java:810` composes the popup from a message key; the key is absent from the shipped language file, so the line renders through the client's missing-key fallback rather than as prose. A port should supply its own string (checklist T1 records what the reference build shows).
35. **The container keeps an aspect list nothing reads.** `common/container/ContainerResearchTable.java:24-31` populates a string array of every aspect tag at construction; nothing else in the tree reads that field. It is the remnant of an aspect-placement design that BETA26 does not implement — the table has no aspect slots; aspect-flavored card state instead lives on five registered themed cards (concentrate, channel, infuse, reactions, and synthesis — `common/config/ConfigResearch.java:255-257`, `:262-263`).
36. **Three theory fields and one flag are dead weight.** The placed-cards and chosen-aids counters are serialized and deserialized but never incremented or read (`api/research/theorycraft/ResearchTableData.java:25-26`, `:95-104`, `:167-175`), and the aid-drawn flag guarding the aid draw is never set, so aid cards can in principle appear more than once per draw sequence (`:239-265`).
37. **The table's client-event sound branch is unreachable in the tree.** `common/tiles/crafting/TileResearchTable.java:194-202` plays the learn sound on a block event of id one, but no caller in the tree raises that event on a research table; the learn sound the player actually hears on knowledge gain comes from the knowledge-gain packet ([rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md), item 19). Treat the branch as dormant. Sibling dormant sets on other surfaces: [cas-03-evidence.md](cas-03-evidence.md), item 28.
38. **Three catalog artifacts are unreachable as shipped.** The analyze card is registered but can never arm: its initializer tests stored observation points through the reader's still-null category field, which resolves that test to a knowledge pool no writer in the tree ever creates, so its candidate list stays empty and the draw discards it (`api/research/theorycraft/CardAnalyze.java:39-51`; the discard `api/research/theorycraft/ResearchTableData.java:299-311`). A truth card is listed inside the live eldritch aid's card set but was never registered as a card, so it is a permanently dead slot in that aid's draw (`common/lib/research/theorycraft/AidBasicEldritch.java:16`; the registration pass `common/config/ConfigResearch.java:242-274`). And the dragon-egg aid and card are referenced by nothing else — never registered, never drawn (`common/lib/research/theorycraft/AidDragonEgg.java:8-17`, `common/lib/research/theorycraft/CardDragonEgg.java:10-11`).

## Rank-1 capture checklist

Reference setup for every step: the clean 1.12.2 profile whose only mods are the BETA26 jar and Baubles (`REF-0002` and the "Environment feasibility" section of [reference-catalog.md](reference-catalog.md)); a fresh creative world with its seed recorded in a new `REF` entry before the session. The operator command surface `/thaumcraft research <player> <list|all|reset|<key>>` and its revoke form, plus `/thaumcraft reload`, is the setup lever between measurements and must not be used during one (`common/lib/CommandThaumcraft.java:50-52`, `:58-107`); the grant form adds the entry and its parents and drives them complete, so partially staged states come from play ([rsr-01-evidence.md](rsr-01-evidence.md), checklist preamble).
