## Reference behavior (source-derived)

### A. The unlock chain

1. **The Eldritch tab is a research category gated by one key, and its page file is parsed with the rest.** The ELDRITCH category registers under the unlock key `UNLOCKELDRITCH` with its aspect price list, tab icon, and background (`common/config/ConfigResearch.java:144`; the register signature in `api/research/ResearchCategories.java:58`, and a one-line shorthand at `:76`), the category list feeds the file parser so the eldritch research file loads by name (`common/config/ConfigResearch.java:127-130`, `:334`), and the tab's whole content is the five entries in that file (`assets/thaumcraft/research/eldritch.json:4`, `:23`, `:41`, `:59`, `:77`). The player-facing tab title is the `UNLOCKELDRITCH` page's own name, "Delving into the Eldritch" (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1287`).

2. **The gate itself is a hidden basics page: scan a void seed with the observation knowledge in hand.** `UNLOCKELDRITCH` sits in BASICS, hidden and spiky, parented on CrimsonRites and UNLOCKAUROMANCY, and its single stage requires OBSERVATION knowledge in BASICS and ELDRITCH plus the completed scan of a void seed (`f_VOIDSEED`), paying warp 5 (`assets/thaumcraft/research/basics.json:263-281`; stage text at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1288-1289`). The `f_VOIDSEED` scan key is registered against the void-seed item (`common/config/ConfigResearch.java:223`), and the seed itself is the rift lane's payout — a collapse pays them out and the void siphon farms them ([wld-03-evidence.md](wld-03-evidence.md) findings 7 and 10). The page's stage 2 text is the model's statement of what a seed proves: that it is not from this reality but from somewhere outside (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1289`).

3. **The crimson key is a consumed curio with a warp gate.** The "Crimson Rites" book is the last variant of the curio item (`common/items/curios/ItemCurio.java:33`; naming at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:15`, tooltip lines at `:583-584`). Using it: when the player's actual warp exceeds twenty it completes the hidden `CrimsonRites` research, adds random ELDRITCH observation and theory knowledge, adds one point of normal warp, five temporary, and a coin-flip permanent point, and it is consumed unless the player is creative; below twenty warp it only prints the failure line, which tells the player the book held nothing but crazed ravings (`common/items/curios/ItemCurio.java:84-100`, `:106-108`; the fail key `fail.crimsonrites` at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1820`). The changelog records the consumption rule as shipped — the book is used up on a successful study (`changelog.txt:181`).

4. **Where the book comes from: the cult's loot table, on a player kill only.** The registered `thaumcraft:cultist` loot table drops one curio with data 6 at a 1.25% base chance plus 1% per looting level, conditional on the kill being by a player, beside a small gold-nugget tail (`assets/thaumcraft/loot_tables/cultist.json:20-33`; the table is registered and returned by the cultist base class, `common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultist.java:57-59`, `:117-119`). The cultist scan page that precedes it is the hidden `!CrimsonCultist` scan entry (`assets/thaumcraft/research/scans.json:59-65`), registered against the cultist class so its subclasses count (`common/config/ConfigResearch.java:164`).

5. **The crimson chain's page shape.** `CrimsonRites` is a hidden, reversed basics page parented on the cultist scan, whose stage 1 requires the book in inventory and whose stage 2 carries the "Opening the Eye" reveal (`assets/thaumcraft/research/basics.json:246-261`; text at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1198-1200`). Two further knowledge keys are granted by the warp lane and have no page anywhere in the tree — `ELDRITCHMINOR` at warp above 25 and `ELDRITCHMAJOR` above 50 are written into player knowledge by the warp event (`common/lib/events/WarpEvents.java:228-233`), and because an unknown key resolves as a trivially-requisite, page-less research (`common/lib/research/ResearchManager.java:70-84`, `:213-220`), the completion stores a key that renders nothing — a dead reference to flag for the port, not a behavior to copy.

6. **The category's five pages, their gates, and their payouts.** `BASEELDRITCH` ("Void Seeds") has stage 1 gated on theory knowledge in BASICS, ALCHEMY, and ELDRITCH and pays warp 2, and stage 2 unlocks the void-ingot crucible recipe and the void junk group (`assets/thaumcraft/research/eldritch.json:4-21`; text at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1790-1792`). Its four children each require ELDRITCH observation and theory and gate on one outer page: Void Siphon on the infusion page (warp 1), Voidseer's Pearl on the curiosity band (warp 3), Void Thaumaturge Armor on the fortress armor (warp 1), and the Primal Crusher on the elemental tools (warp 1) (`assets/thaumcraft/research/eldritch.json:23-39`, `:41-57`, `:59-75`, `:77-93`; titles at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1794`, `:1798`, `:1802`, `:1806`). Each stage 2 names the recipes it unlocks — `thaumcraft:VoidSiphon`, `thaumcraft:VoidseerPearl`, the three void-robe pieces, `thaumcraft:PrimalCrusher` (`assets/thaumcraft/research/eldritch.json:36`, `:54`, `:72`, `:90`).

7. **Where ELDRITCH knowledge comes from.** The eldritch curio (data 3) grants ELDRITCH observation and theory plus one normal and five temporary warp, and the eldritch-themed theorycraft cards feed the category's totals at the research table (`common/items/curios/ItemCurio.java:66-71`; `common/lib/research/theorycraft/CardDarkWhispers.java:27`, `:52`; `common/lib/research/theorycraft/CardRevelation.java:21`, `:38`; `common/lib/research/theorycraft/CardCurio.java:100-113`). ### B. Structures and worldgen

8. **The only Eldritch-land generation in the overworld: a one-in-a-hundred barrow and a one-in-five-hundred roaming portal, both behind one config flag.** The surface pass, gated on the `generateStructure` flag and a non-flat overworld, rolls one in a hundred for a mound at a jittered chunk centre nine blocks down from the precipitation height, and otherwise one in five hundred for a lesser crimson portal placed eight blocks above that same sampled cell — which puts its footing at the precipitation height — when the block below is ground, rock, sand, or snow (`common/world/ThaumcraftWorldGenerator.java:71-94`; config defaults `common/config/ModConfig.java:382` and regeneration flag `:390`). The mound is a hand-built barrow generator: it validates a nineteen-block-square footprint by probing its corners and refuses on bad ground (`common/world/objects/WorldGenMound.java:27-60`), and its crypt itemization is a chest with a vanilla simple-dungeon loot table, a one-in-three trap variant that places a trapped chest over TNT, and three-tier loot crates or urns placed around the chamber (`:2496-2543`), plus a skeleton spawner and a zombie spawner at its (4,5,4) and (4,5,14) corners (`:2544-2553`).

9. **The crates and urns are one block class in six skins, and they are the rarity faucet.** Each is a breakable, silk-harvestable block whose drop table pulls `1 + tier + rand(3)` draws from the shared weighted loot lists by tier (`common/blocks/world/BlockLoot.java:23-29`, `:43-45`, `:58-67`), registered as common/uncommon/rare crates and urns (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:248-253`), and the draws resolve through the same rarity switch that fills loot bags (`common/lib/utils/Utils.java:305-325`).

10. **The Eldritch structure block family is named but not built: altar, obelisk base and body, capstone, locking mechanism, doorway, portal, and crab spawning opening all exist as language-file names only.** The block behind them, `BlocksTC.eldritch`, is declared (`api/blocks/BlocksTC.java:72`) and referenced by the aspect table for its eight metadata values (`common/config/ConfigAspects.java:566-573`), by the cleric's altar-focus AI (`common/entities/ai/misc/AIAltarFocus.java:36`), and by the eldritch research aid (`common/lib/research/theorycraft/AidBasicEldritch.java:11`), but it is never assigned anywhere in the tree — a whole-tree search for an assignment finds none — so every reference is inert, and no generator builds the structures: a whole-tree search for structure components and map-gen classes finds only the mound and the tree generators. The creative placer's obelisk variant clears the clicked cell to air and returns success without building anything (`common/items/misc/ItemCreativePlacer.java:23`, `:32-54`), and the "Eldritch Eye" item exists only as a name and an aspect tag (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:582`; `common/config/ConfigAspects.java:555`) plus a Pech trade slot (`common/config/ConfigEntities.java:189`). The block-state assets for the ancient family that does have sub-blocks are present in the resource tree (`assets/thaumcraft/blockstates/stone_ancient_doorway.json` etc.), which is the shape a port will need if the family is restored.

11. **The greater portal carries the only structure-writing behavior in the cult: it builds its own arena dressing.** On arrival it plants four crimson cult banners six blocks out on the cardinals with arc FX, then scatters loot crates across the three tiers in a five-block spread while the fight opens, each placement flashing block-arc FX (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityCultistPortalGreater.java:115-146`, `:147-170`); the crate blocks it places are the registered loot crates (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:248-250`). But nothing in the tree spawns this entity (finding 15), so the dressing is operator-only.

### C. The cult in play

12. **The lesser crimson portal is the reachable portal boss, and it kills itself through its own waves.** It is a hundred-health, zero-attack, fixed-position crimson portal that never despawns and wakes only when a player comes within thirty-two blocks, after which it spawns one knight-or-cleric wave each time its timer expires, capped by difficulty (two, four, or six) minus the cultists already within thirty-two blocks, and every wave costs the portal five to nine of its own health — so a player who clears the waves ends the portal (`common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistPortalLesser.java:61-70`, `:114-140`, `:166`). Its active flag is data-synced and saved, and it collides for four damage while a player touches it; its death makes a power-1.5 explosion and drops nothing (`:48-59`, `:72-80`, `:173-177`, `:203-204`, `:222-227`). It reaches a world two ways: the one-in-five-hundred surface roll (finding 8) and the warp event, where an effect roll of 81 through 88 spawns one near the player after up to fifty placement attempts and prints the chanting line (`common/lib/events/WarpEvents.java:214-216`, `:248-265`; the line at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:489`). The warp event itself runs every two thousand player ticks, only with warp above zero, only outside wuss mode, and not under the warp-ward effect (`common/lib/events/PlayerEvents.java:328-331`; the warp gate `common/lib/events/WarpEvents.java:59`).

13. **The two cultist ranks that fight beside a portal.** The knight is thirty health with melee, the crimson plate set, and a rare upgrade roll that swaps in a void sword with a robe hood (one in five of a one-to-five-percent chance by difficulty); the cleric is twenty-four health, ranged with golem orbs or a three-fireball burst, dressed in the crimson robe with a boots roll (`common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistKnight.java:50-86`; `common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistCleric.java:76-115`). Both are team-locked to cultists and leaders and hostile to eldritch guardians and illagers, and both inherit the base line — speed 0.3, attack 4, follow range 32, ten experience, and the empty drop hooks — with the knight adding its own weapon-enchantment roll on spawn (`common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultist.java:26`, `:34-39`, `:61-65`, `:94-100`; `common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistKnight.java:44-47`, `:80-86`; `common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistCleric.java:70-73`). Full AI and drop validation is `WLD-05`'s; the scan side is this lane's (finding 4).

14. **The cleric's ritualist flag belongs to the dead altar content.** The flag is data-synced, saved, and disables its despawn; it is cleared by damage or when the altar-focus AI finds the home position is not an `eldritch` block — inert, because that block is unassigned (finding 10) (`common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistCleric.java:117-120`, `:122-141`, `:144-153`; `common/entities/ai/misc/AIAltarFocus.java:21-38`). The cleric also mirrors its home position to watching clients as spawn data and faces it while a ritualist (`common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistCleric.java:155-176`).

### D. The bosses and their arenas

15. **Four boss classes are registered, rendered, and named — and never spawned by any code path in this tree.** Whole-tree searches for constructions find none for `EntityEldritchWarden`, `EntityEldritchGolem`, `EntityTaintacleGiant`, or `EntityCultistPortalGreater`; they exist in the entity registry (`common/config/ConfigEntities.java:82`, `:102-105`), their renderers, and their aspect tags — the greater portal excepted, which carries no tag (`common/config/ConfigAspects.java:120-123`) — while the warden, the construct, and the portal also sit on the safari-net blacklist and the giant taintacle does not (`common/config/ModConfig.java:222-235`) — so in this tree they are operator content (`/summon`), and the four boss-room announcement lines on the keys `tc.boss.golem`, `tc.boss.warden`, `tc.boss.taint`, and `tc.boss.crimson` are referenced by no code (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:559-562`).

16. **The boss base class supplies the bar, the leash, and the multiplayer scaling.** `EntityThaumcraftBoss` carries a purple progress bar that darkens the sky, persists its home position in NBT, is invulnerable while its spawn timer runs, never despawns, counts eldritch mobs as allies, drops a primordial pearl plus a rare loot bag, and enrages when a single hit exceeds thirty-five — regenerating, strengthening, and hasting for ten seconds scaled by the blow, with the hit capped at thirty-five (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityThaumcraftBoss.java:44`, `:50-65`, `:123-125`, `:186-188`, `:212-218`, `:220-223`, `:225-255`). Multiplayer scaling lives in its tick: an aggro map accumulates damage per attacker, a pass every twenty ticks retargets to the top contributor when that attacker leads by more than twenty-five points and ten percent, and then health and damage modifiers are applied for every extra attacking player up to five — the stacks are flat additions of fifty max health and half a point of damage per extra attacker, up to five, not percentages, so their effective share varies with the boss's base health (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityThaumcraftBoss.java:225-234`, `:139-182`; `common/lib/utils/EntityUtils.java:441-442`).

17. **The Eldritch Warden is the Outer Lands' set-piece: two boss bars, a shield phase, a ranged mix, and a field-control frenzy.** Four hundred health, ten damage, 0.33 speed, and a fifteen-name title list whose pick is saved as a byte (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityEldritchWarden.java:129-134`, `:78`, `:113-126`, `:143-151`). Its second, blue notched-ten bar tracks an absorption shield worth two-thirds of base health that refills a point per twenty-five ticks while the entity is not in hurt-resistance time (`:77`, `:163-167`); it spawns frozen with a hundred-and-fifty-tick timer, invulnerable while it ticks, with a smoke-spiral FX to clients and a single state packet fired at the freeze's start — sent once, while the spawn timer still reads its full hundred and fifty (`:172-193` with the packet at `:172-174` and the timer set at `:319-324`, `:301-303`). Its ranged attack is eighty percent eldritch orbs — two-thirds of its attack value on impact plus eight seconds of weakness (`common/entities/projectile/EntityEldritchOrb.java:37-54`) — and twenty percent a sonic screech that knocks the target back, applies twenty seconds of wither and weakness, and adds three to five temporary warp (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityEldritchWarden.java:330-359`). Breaking the shield once triggers the frenzy: a hundred-and-fifty-tick countdown that starts by teleporting it home and then paints expanding rings of its sap effect over the ground each ten ticks — up to roughly eighteen blocks out, a third of the placements flinging block-arc FX, each ring playing a zap (`:217-241`, `:243-298`); it also seeds sap blocks under its own feet while walking and cannot attack eldritch guardians (`:195-215`, `:382-384`). Its drop is the base class's pearl and rare bag (finding 16).

18. **The Greater Crimson Portal is the unreachable finale: a five-hundred-health fixed portal that fights through staged waves and self-destructs as its cult dies.** It is immovable, fire-immune, armored five, with a red notched-six bar and its stage saved (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityCultistPortalGreater.java:47-60`, `:66-74`, `:83-92`). After the banner-and-crate staging (finding 11), a stage machine runs while a player is within forty-eight blocks: it spawns knight-or-cleric minions each cycle, the praetor arrives at stage twelve, and every wave after that also costs the portal five to nine health — and each wave's timing grows with the number of cultists alive within thirty-two blocks, so the fight accelerates as the ranks thin (`:172-213`, `:229-231`). It ignores potion effects and fall damage, collides for eight damage on contact, and its death makes a power-2 explosion and drops a primordial pearl (`:244-248`, `:288-299`, `:274-276`).

19. **The Praetor leads the cult.** The leader is a hundred-and-fifty-health, five-damage boss whose ranged golem-orb attack engages beyond sixteen blocks — that figure is the long-range AI's minimum distance, refusing closer targets, under a twenty-four-block attack reach (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityCultistLeader.java:62`) — with praetor armor plus a void sword on easy or a crimson blade otherwise with a random enchantment roll, and an aura that refreshes short regeneration on every cultist within eight blocks (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityCultistLeader.java:72-78`, `:114-133`, `:176-193`, `:161-174`). It is team-locked to cultists and cannot target the cult ranks, and it drops a rare loot bag (`:136-142`, `:148-151`). Its only spawner is the greater portal (finding 15).

20. **The Eldritch Construct is a two-phase sentinel that beheads once before it fights.** Four hundred health, ten damage, six extra armor, fire-immune, at 1.75 by 3.5 blocks (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityEldritchGolem.java:59-69`, `:123-132`). It spawns with a hundred-tick timer and heals two per tick while it runs — the "frozen sentinel" the unused message describes (`:147-151`, `:264-271`; the message at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:560`). The first blow that would kill it instead takes its head: the hit is refused, a power-2 explosion fires at head height, the timer restarts, and it gains the ranged task, after which it charges a hundred-and-fifty-tick beam and fires golem orbs in bursts (`:176-192`, `:210-228`, `:292-303`). While moving it grinds any loot block it stands on into dropped items and destroys anything with hardness at or below 0.15 (`:153-173`).

21. **The Giant Taintacle is a boss-tier taintacle.** It extends the full tentacle as a one-hundred-and-seventy-five-health, nine-damage pillar at 1.1 by 6.0 blocks with its own purple bar, twenty experience, no despawn, and breath-holding, championed on spawn and unable to naturally spawn, healing a point per thirty ticks and capping any single hit at thirty-five while enraging like the base boss (`common/entities/monster/boss/EntityTaintacleGiant.java:31-48`, `:50-53`, `:86-108`, `:67-69`, `:110-127`). It drops a primordial pearl only when no other giant is within forty-eight blocks (`:91-96`). The champion whitelist line for taintacles sits with the rest of the champion roster (`common/config/ConfigEntities.java:246`), and its inherited taint-bound behavior — spawning onto taint material, starving off it, and seeding small tentacles toward a target — is the full tentacle's, derived in [wld-03-evidence.md](wld-03-evidence.md) finding 17.

### E. The dimension and the rest of the dead endgame

22. **The Outer Lands are named but not implemented: an id, a biome, and no way in.** The dimension id is configuration with default minus forty-two (`common/config/ModConfig.java:330-332`, `:375`); the "Outer Lands" biome is constructed and registry-registered, with its spawn lists replaced by the inhabited zombie and the eldritch guardian, dirt top and filler, a black sky color, and an empty decorator (`Registrar.java:112-113`; `common/world/biomes/BiomeGenEldritch.java:15-34`), and it is typed magical, spooky, and end (`common/world/biomes/BiomeHandler.java:106`); but it is never added to the biome manager — only the magical forest is (`Registrar.java:115-118`). No world-provider class exists in the tree, no dimension is registered anywhere (no registration call in the tree), and the world generator's only response to the dimension id is to mark its chunks dirty without generating anything (`common/world/ThaumcraftWorldGenerator.java:53-56`). The portal blocks that would carry a player in are the unassigned family of finding 10, so in this tree there is no entry, no exit, and no respawn behavior to derive.

23. **Four behaviors still read the dimension id as if it were a place.** The generator's skip (finding 22); the guardian, which gains an absorption shield of half its fifty base health on spawn in the dimension and refills it one point per twenty-five ticks while inside (`common/entities/monster/EntityEldritchGuardian.java:190-208`, the shield at `:194` and `:203`), and which sends a distant-player misc packet while outside it (`:124-136`); the anti-flight rule, which cuts flight every second in the dimension for non-creative players with the flight-disruption line (`common/lib/events/PlayerEvents.java:176-180`; `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1085`); and the rift focus, which refuses to place its hole in the dimension (`common/items/casters/foci/FocusEffectRift.java:57`). The changelog's line for the tier says it is an early fragment of the eldritch research with more planned, which matches the code's state (`changelog.txt:103`).

24. **The wardens, constructs, portals, and taintacle giants are the remnants of the dimension's planned encounter set.** Their announce lines are the four "room" messages (finding 15), their arenas are the obelisk/altar structures that were never generated (finding 10), and their only presence in a live world is a summon. The effect route that does reach players — the guardian war, the mind spider — is the warp event's: mist with guardian escorts, sudden real or illusion spiders, and the portal (findings 12, 30). Cross-references, not derivations: the warp consequences themselves are `PLY-01`'s lane, and the mist, spider, and guardian spawn helpers are bounded to their cited lines here (`common/lib/events/WarpEvents.java:237-316`).

### F. Endgame unlocks, config gates, and multiplayer

25. **What the eldritch pages pay out, in dependency order.** `BASEELDRITCH` opens the void-metal pair: a crucible recipe that turns a void seed plus ten metal and five flux into a void ingot (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:143`), and the `void_stuff` crafting group that carries ingots into blocks, plates, a full tool set, and armor with a dye recipe beside it (`:376`, `:379`, `:414-423`). `VOIDSIPHON` is an infusion of a void block and a nether star (`:369`); `VOIDSEERPEARL` an infusion of a rare bauble with two brains, a seed, and a primordial pearl under mind, void, and magic (`:370`); `VOIDROBEARMOR` the three void-robe infusions built on the void armor pieces (`:341-343`), whose product is a goggles-and-revealer armor with dye support and three warp — the helm is the goggles (`common/items/armor/ItemVoidRobeArmor.java:38`, `:102-107`, `:127-161`, `:202-204`; text at `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1804`); `PRIMALCRUSHER` the pearl-and-tools infusion making a self-repairing pickaxe-and-shovel with a taint-inclusive effective set, twenty enchantability, and two warp (`common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:351`; `common/items/tools/ItemPrimalCrusher.java:30-36`, `:63-73`, `:88-97`, `:108-110`). `INFUSIONELDRITCH` — the eldritch infusion altar multiblock and its pedestal, gated on scanning the eldritch tile — carries the recipes for the eldritch pedestal and altar (`assets/thaumcraft/research/infusion.json:98-116`; `common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:102-110`, `:232`).

26. **The warp price list the player pays for all of it.** Research-stage warp is paid at completion with a permanent/normal split and is skipped wholesale in wuss mode (`common/lib/research/ResearchManager.java:130-153`; the stage accessor at `api/research/ResearchStage.java:125`): the unlock page costs 5 (`assets/thaumcraft/research/basics.json:275`), Base Eldritch 2 (`assets/thaumcraft/research/eldritch.json:14`), the Siphon 1 (`:32`), the Pearl 3 (`:50`), the Robe 1 (`:68`), the Crusher 1 (`:86`), and the eldritch altar 2 (`assets/thaumcraft/research/infusion.json:113`). The gear adds warp while carried through the warping-gear interface and a `TC.WARP` tag read (`common/lib/events/PlayerEvents.java:287-300`): void tools and armor one each (`common/items/tools/ItemVoidSword.java:74-76`; `common/items/armor/ItemVoidArmor.java:84-86`), the void robe three (`common/items/armor/ItemVoidRobeArmor.java:202-204`), the crimson blade two (`common/items/tools/ItemCrimsonBlade.java:84-86`), cultist robes and boots one each (`common/items/armor/ItemCultistRobeArmor.java:88-90`; `common/items/armor/ItemCultistBoots.java:65-67`), the primal crusher two (`common/items/tools/ItemPrimalCrusher.java:88-90`), and the voidseer charm a dynamic zero to five from its own discount (`common/items/baubles/ItemVoidseerCharm.java:54-56`). Warp itself is `PLY-01`'s; this lane records only what the eldritch gear charges.

27. **The one endgame payout that scales with warp is the Voidseer's Pearl.** Its charm returns up to twenty-five percent vis discount scaled by permanent warp, capped at a hundred warp, and its carried warp is a fifth of that discount (`common/items/baubles/ItemVoidseerCharm.java:44-56`); the page states the model — the bonus grows with permanent warp (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1800`) — and its flavor line describes peering into darkness and seeing something staring back (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:187`).

28. **The loot that feeds the loop.** The crates and urns draw the shared weighted rarity lists (finding 9), and those lists — the same ones loot bags use — include in the rare tier a nether star, five pearl damage values including the top one at weight one, and the rare baubles, with primordial pearls seeded in every tier and the void-themed items absent from the lists entirely (`common/config/ModConfig.java:67-114`, with the nether star at `:82`, the top pearl at `:81`, and the rare bauble at `:93`). The Eldritch Eye's only faucet is the Pech's arcane trade (finding 10).

29. **Config gates and the two dead gate items.** `generateStructure` (default true) gates the barrow and roaming-portal pass and `regenStructure` (default false) its re-run (`common/config/ModConfig.java:382`, `:390`; gate at `common/world/ThaumcraftWorldGenerator.java:73`); `wussMode` (default false) removes both the warp event — and with it every spawn of the lesser portal, the guardians, and the spiders outside worldgen — and the research warp payouts (`common/config/ModConfig.java:412-413`, `:428`; `common/lib/events/PlayerEvents.java:328-331`; `common/lib/research/ResearchManager.java:139`); `allowChampionMobs` (default true) feeds the champion roll the giant taintacle is forced through and that general mobs get, with the dimension and spooky-biome discounts applied (`common/config/ModConfig.java:400-402`; `common/lib/events/EntityEvents.java:275-295`; `common/entities/monster/boss/EntityTaintacleGiant.java:51`). Nothing else about this lane is configurable: there is no flag for the biome, the obelisk family, or the cult, and the two gate items of the infusion altars — ancient stone and the eldritch tile — have no recipe and no generator in this tree (recipes only consume them: `common/config/ConfigRecipes.java:93-101`, `:231-232`, `:451`, `:458-459`), which is why the two altar pages' stage-1 item requirements (`assets/thaumcraft/research/infusion.json:89`, `:108`) and the scan keys behind them (`common/config/ConfigResearch.java:186-188`) cannot be satisfied by normal play as this tree reads — the T3/T8 probes record the attempt.

30. **Multiplayer, reload, and the client-visible state.** The lesser portal's active flag and the cleric's ritualist flag are data-managed, saved, and mirrored to clients (`common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistPortalLesser.java:29`, `:48-59`, `:72-80`; `common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistCleric.java:122-153`), and the portal's wave logic runs server-side only (`common/entities/monster/cult/EntityCultistPortalLesser.java:114`). The eldritch crab's mount link is a synced id with a client-side reconcile — but only its helm bit is written to NBT, so a reloaded crab returns unmounted, its rider id re-initialized empty, and the reconcile matters only while a server-set id is live in the current session (`common/entities/monster/EntityEldritchCrab.java:46-47`, `:80-92` — the reset at `:83` — `:132-164`, `:195-206`), which is the T8 probe. Boss home positions, the Warden's title, the Construct's headless state, and the greater portal's stage are all saved (findings 16-20). The mind spider's harmless-and-viewer pair is synced and saved, harmless spiders expire after twelve hundred ticks and deal no damage (`common/entities/monster/EntityMindSpider.java:53-74`, `:80-85`, `:106-116`). The mark that unites the eldritch roster is a bare marker interface used by seven classes and by two scan keys that no research page consumes (`api/entities/IEldritchMob.java:1-6`; `common/config/ConfigResearch.java:184-185`; scan keys `!ORMOB` and `!ORBOSS` appear in no research file in the tree).

## Rank-1 capture checklist

Reference setup for every step: the clean 1.12.2 profile whose only mods are the BETA26 jar and Baubles (`REF-0002` and the "Environment feasibility" section of [reference-catalog.md](reference-catalog.md)); a fresh creative world with its seed recorded in a new `REF` entry before the session. Carry the Thaumometer as the scanning tool, and use the operator levers between measurements only, never during one, recording each use in the `REF` entry: `/thaumcraft reload` and `/thaumcraft research <player> <list|all|reset|<key>>` (`common/lib/CommandThaumcraft.java:64-70`, `:84-108`), the warp lever `/thaumcraft warp <player> <add|set> <amount> <PERM|TEMP>` (`:74-79`), and `/summon` for the four unreachable bosses and the cultists, whose registry ids are the class names in `common/config/ConfigEntities.java:82-118`. There is no dimension-teleport lever to use: the Outer Lands dimension is not registered in this tree (finding 22), so T8 records that attempt as not-applicable rather than forcing it. Start every structure and arena from a recorded position and a screenshot so reload steps can compare exact cells.

- **T1 — The cult scan and the book.** Spawn a cultist wave (an operator `/summon` of a knight or cleric, or a found worldgen portal), scan one and record the `!CrimsonCultist` page appearing; then kill cultists and record the book drop, or `/give` the curio at data 6 as a fallback and record that as operator assistance. Study the book below twenty actual warp and record the failure line; then set warp above twenty and study again, recording the research completion, the knowledge gain, and the warp deltas (findings 3-5, 13). Bound: one wave, one book, one warp setting.
- **T2 — The void-seed gate.** Obtain a void seed (a rift collapse or the siphon, [wld-03-evidence.md](wld-03-evidence.md) findings 7 and 10, or `/give`), scan it, and record the `UNLOCKELDRITCH` completion, the five-point warp payout, and the ELDRITCH tab appearing in the Thaumonomicon; record that no earlier action opens the tab (findings 1-2, 26). Bound: one seed, one scan, one tab check.
- **T3 — The eldritch pages and the void-metal economy.** With the tab open, complete all five pages, recording each stage's gate and warp cost against finding 26; then produce a void ingot in a crucible from a seed and record the aspects charged; craft the void tools and armor from the group recipes; and run the four infusions — siphon, voidseer pearl, robe set, primal crusher — recording outputs and costs (finding 25). Then attempt the eldritch altar: record the scan-key and item requirements and whether the eldritch tile can be obtained without commands (finding 29); treat the attempt, not the result, as the measurement. Bound: one of each craft, one altar attempt.
- **T4 — The lesser portal fight (the reachable boss).** At a found portal, record wake-on-approach, the wave count and composition against difficulty, the local-cultist cap, the portal's self-damage per wave, the contact damage, and the death explosion with no drops (findings 8, 12). Bound: one portal, three waves, one death.
- **T5 — The Greater Portal and the Praetor (operator-summoned).** Summon the greater portal and record its bar and immobility, the banner and crate staging, the minion waves with their timing growth as the cult dies, the praetor's arrival at stage twelve, the praetor's aura and blade, and the portal's pearl drop on death (findings 11, 18-19). Bound: one portal, one leader, one pearl count.
- **T6 — The Eldritch Warden (operator-summoned).** Summon the warden and record the two bars, the spawn freeze and its smoke, the absorption bar's refill and drop, the orb and sonic split with the sonic's warp amount on a test player, the frenzy teleport and ring pattern with the sap blocks, the guardian truce in a two-warden test if the operator accepts it, and the drop against finding 16 (finding 17). Bound: one warden, one frenzy, one pearl count.
- **T7 — The dormant pair and the obelisk family.** Summon the Eldritch Construct and record the frozen timer, the beheading first blow with its explosion and refused damage, and the headless beam; summon the Giant Taintacle and record champion status, the thirty-five cap and enrage, the pearl-alone rule with a second giant, and its taint-bound behavior against [wld-03-evidence.md](wld-03-evidence.md) finding 17; then probe the obelisk family with the creative placer and the blocks themselves and record that nothing is built and the block is unobtainable (findings 10, 20-21). Bound: one of each boss, one placer use, one block probe.
- **T8 — Structures, the dimension, reload, and a second client.** With the seed recorded, sweep a bounded set of chunks around spawn for barrows and roaming portals and record the observed rates against the one-in-a-hundred and one-in-five-hundred rolls (finding 8). Attempt the dimension: record that the id is configured and that no command or block reaches it (finding 22), and note the flight and rift-focus checks as unobservable here (finding 23). Reload probes: save and re-enter with a summoned warden, construct, portal, and crab in place, and record title, headless state, stage, home leash, and the crab's mount against finding 30. Second client on a LAN: compare the boss bars, the portal's active state, the cleric's ritualist facing, and the warden's ring FX across both clients, and record the multiplayer health/damage stacks with two attackers against finding 16. Bound: one chunk sweep, four reload checks, one co-op pass.
