## Reference behavior (source-derived)

### A. The tube family and its block surface

1. **Six tube variants share one block class and one base tile, and each variant is a small override.** The blocks are registered as `tube`, `tube_valve`, `tube_restrict`, `tube_oneway`, `tube_filter`, and `tube_buffer`, each with its own tile class (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:318-323`; tiles `:382-387`), and the tree's language file names them the Essentia Tube, Essentia Valve, Filtered Essentia Tube, Restricted Essentia Tube, Directional Essentia Tube, and Essentia Buffer (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:324-329`). The base tile declares the transport contract, the caster interaction, and the ticker (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:35`); the variants change only the suction scan (restrict, oneway, filter), the flow flag (valve), or storage and suction (buffer). Two further transport blocks complete the family — `essentia_input` and `essentia_output` (`common/config/ConfigBlocks.java:337-338`), the Filling and Emptying Essentia Transfusers (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:342-343`) — and are itemized in item 15.
2. **A tube's surface is six open sides that a caster can rewire.** All six sides start open (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:48-58`) and the transport contract answers connectable, input, and output from exactly that flag (`:236-248`); placing the block sets the variant's facing from the placer (`common/blocks/essentia/BlockTube.java:102-109`); a caster click on a face toggles it and mirrors the toggle to the neighbouring tube, while a click on the centre rotates the facing to the next open side with a connectable neighbour (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:331-368`; the resonator only reads and never toggles, `common/items/tools/ItemResonator.java:44-79`); the drawn connection state comes from the connectable-neighbour checks (`common/blocks/essentia/BlockTube.java:111-130`), and the per-side click cuboids follow that same connectable-neighbour test rather than the open-side flag (`:153-194`, `:278-293`; `common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:384-406`).
3. **A tube holds and moves exactly one unit of one aspect.** An addition is accepted only into an empty tube and a take only for the matching aspect from an output-enabled face (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:276-298`), minimum suction is zero (`:300-303`), and the client payload carries the unit, facing, and open sides (`:61-86`) while suction is written to the tile's saved form (`:88-103`; the shared save, sync, and update-tag shapes are `common/tiles/TileThaumcraft.java:42-75`).

### B. Suction, travel, congestion, and the variants that shape them

4. **Suction is inherited from the strongest neighbour, not generated.** Every second tick, while the tube is not venting, it scans its connectable sides and adopts the largest neighbour suction that exceeds its current value by more than one — storing one less than that neighbour, or half of it for the restricted variant — with the neighbour's suction type (or the tube's own filter) as its advertised type (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:105-124`, `:137-169`, `:155-159`). Because the update requires a strictly greater raw value, equal neighbours do not displace the first one for the plain tube, and a chain of tubes steps its suction down by one per hop, so flow points at the strongest advertised pull. The halving variant compares that raw value against its own running stored value, so there a later, smaller neighbour can displace a larger half and the settled value depends on face order — ten, then twenty, then fifteen settles a plain tube at nineteen and a restricted one at seven. The tree's own text agrees at a player level: the highest drawing strength takes priority and a tube carries one kind of essentia at a time (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1379`).
5. **Travel is a pull, one unit per attempt, behind four gates.** An empty, non-venting tube scans in face order and takes one unit from the first side that passes: the neighbour must be connectable and allow output on the shared face; the tube's suction must strictly exceed the neighbour's and meet the neighbour's minimum; and the advertised types must be compatible (an empty side or a matching type) (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:196-233`); a successful pull stores the unit and rolls a creak sound at one percent (`:218-224`). Every hop is therefore a pull toward the higher-suction side, once per attempt, and no tube can pass on more than it holds.
6. **Conflicting pulls are vented, not lost.** When a compatible neighbour advertises a suction equal to the tube's own or one less with a different type — and is not a filter tube — the tube fires a vent block event and pauses its suction scan and transfers for forty ticks (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:171-194`, `:106-124`); clients hear a hiss and draw particles coloured by the contested suction type (`:126-134`, `:305-328`), matching the tree's description that vented gas marks interfering suctions (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1379`). Nothing is destroyed by venting; the loss paths this note derives are deliberate rather than incidental — the void jar's discard (item 12), the break spill (item 17), and the mirror's instability bleed (item 13).
7. **The valve cuts flow by letting its advertised suction collapse to zero.** Its connectability excludes the facing side, so that side never connects to anything (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTubeValve.java:113-116`), and while flow is disallowed the tile refuses to store new suction values (`:118-123`) — but its periodic scan still runs on the base tube's two-tick cadence (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:113-115`) and starts by writing its suction and suction type to zero and null directly (`:137-139`), which the refusal cannot undo. A closed valve therefore advertises nothing by its next scan and can neither draw against a live gradient nor rely on holding its unit; whether the shipped build shows even the short transient before that collapse is a rank-1 check (T3). Redstone power toggles the flow flag on the five-tick cadence with a squeak, and the block also toggles it on a hand click unless a caster, resonator, or tube is held (`:29-46`; `common/blocks/essentia/BlockTube.java:228-243`).
8. **The directional tube fixes the suction side, and the restricted tube halves the gradient.** The directional variant forces directional mode in both loops (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTubeOneway.java:7-15`): suction is accepted only from the face opposite the block's facing (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:144`) and units are never taken from that face (`:204`), so the only downstream direction is toward the suction face — the tree's blue-marked side (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1379`). The restricted variant halves every value it inherits (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTubeRestrict.java:7-10` with the halving at `common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:158`), which attenuates a chain rather than cutting it.

### C. Filters, labels, and the jar-side gates

9. **A filter tube types its whole segment of the chain.** It runs the suction scan with its stored filter as the required type (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTubeFilter.java:31-34`), so only neighbours whose advertised suction type is empty or equal to the filter can raise its suction, and it advertises the filter as its own type (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:149-157`); it is also exempt from the venting trigger, so a differently typed pull does not trip it (`:181`), and its unit pulls use the same type test against the neighbouring side (`:210`). The filter is set by using an essentia container item on the block — its first aspect — and cleared by a sneak click whenever a filter is set (the clear branch tests only the sneak and the set filter, not an empty hand; `common/blocks/essentia/BlockTube.java:244-266`), it syncs with the tube (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTubeFilter.java:16-29`), and container overlays can display it because the tile answers with a single entry whose amount is minus one while every real container operation refuses (`:36-81`).
10. **The buffer is a small mixed store with bellows-driven suction and a per-side choke.** It keeps an aspect list capped at ten total units and admits one unit per addition (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTubeBuffer.java:25-28`, `:78-90`), exposes it as a container and through the transport reads (`:69-72`, `:157-170`), and scales its comparator over that ten (`common/blocks/essentia/BlockTube.java:196-209`). Suction is per side: zero when the side is choked closed, one when it is choked low or no bellows is counted, and thirty-two per counted bellows otherwise (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTubeBuffer.java:157-160`); sneak clicks cycle a side's choke and plain clicks toggle it (`:248-285`); the buffer refuses to give a unit away when a competing side advertises a stronger compatible pull (`:172-204`) and refills itself one unit per five ticks while below capacity (`:211-224`); the shared bellows count is `common/tiles/devices/TileBellows.java:82-94`. Its language text says eight points and describes the blue and red choke bands (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1379`) while the code caps at ten — which the shipped jar does is **unverified — needs rank-1 observation** (T6).
11. **Jars gate transport through their label, and only their top face connects.** The jar's transport faces are the single top face (`common/tiles/essentia/TileJarFillable.java:126-139`); its pull numbers and cadence are borrowed for direction in [alc-01-evidence.md](alc-01-evidence.md) item 24 and are not re-derived here, and the alembic's output faces are [alc-01-evidence.md](alc-01-evidence.md) items 19-21. What this note adds: the label gate on the accept test (`common/tiles/essentia/TileJarFillable.java:122-124`) and its player path (`common/blocks/essentia/BlockJar.java:214-232`), the brass seal (`:178-183`), label removal with the item returned by sneaking on the label face (`:186-196`), manual emptying that converts the contents to aura pollution (`:197-210`), and breaking that preserves the contents in the item because the jar suppresses the shared spill for that moment (`:79-84`, `:111-126`). A sealed jar is skipped by handler drains (`common/lib/events/EssentiaHandler.java:44-53`) yet is not refused by a tube, because the jar's own pull path never tests the seal (`common/tiles/essentia/TileJarFillable.java:186-214`) — consistent with the tree's text that braces stop everything except tubes (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1348`).
12. **The void jar is the jar variant with its own numbers and a discard rule.** Its filtered suction reads 48 — below the base jar's 64, above the void jar's own unfiltered 32 — and it accepts matching additions past its cap by discarding the excess, with a rare pollution roll as the only cost and a hard clamp at 250 (`common/tiles/essentia/TileJarFillableVoid.java:16-51`). Everything else about jars is item 11 and [alc-01-evidence.md](alc-01-evidence.md).

### D. Mirrors

13. **An essentia mirror is a handler-managed endpoint, not a tube endpoint.** The tile implements only the source contract, never the transport contract (`common/tiles/devices/TileMirrorEssentia.java:20`), and tubes only ever see transport tiles (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:379-382`; `api/ThaumcraftApiHelper.java:69-82`), so no tube can connect to a mirror. Its add, take, containment, and acceptance calls all run the shared handler against the partner's block with range eight, always skipping mirrors (`common/tiles/devices/TileMirrorEssentia.java:189-196`, `:198-215`, `:217-234`, `:240-250`; the handler operations are [fnd-04-evidence.md](fnd-04-evidence.md) item 26 and are not restated). Every unit that crosses raises that mirror's own instability counter — the helper can also forward the increment to the partner, but this tile's two call sites both pass no world (`:60-74`, `:210`, `:229`); past sixty-four instability the tile pollutes the aura in batches and decays the rest one per hundred ticks (`:277-286`).
14. **Mirror pairs are made with mirror items, need support, and repair themselves.** Using a mirror item on a placed unlinked mirror returns it linked to that spot, and placing a linked item restores the pair in both directions (`common/blocks/devices/BlockMirrorItem.java:38-92`, `:94-119`; the tile's restore path is `common/tiles/devices/TileMirrorEssentia.java:76-98`); breaking a mirror drops the linked item back and invalidates the partner (`common/blocks/devices/BlockMirror.java:144-175`); a mirror needs a solid side behind it and drops if that disappears (`:84-91`, `:117-119`); the block's own activation is a stub (`:121-123`), and the hand mirror only links the item mirror (`common/items/tools/ItemHandMirror.java:56-72`). The tile retries an invalid link every forty ticks, backing off to six hundred while it keeps failing (`common/tiles/devices/TileMirrorEssentia.java:260-275`), and a link is valid only while the partner dimension is loaded and both ends point at each other (`:119-164`).

### E. The rest of the family, failure, and reload

15. **The two transfusers are single-face relays with no storage.** `essentia_input` (the Filling Transfuser) advertises a fixed 128 suction on its one connectable face, pushes a unit through the handler into sources within sixteen blocks of its facing direction, and then takes the unit out of the connected transport to match (`common/tiles/essentia/TileEssentiaInput.java:20-33`, `:49-52`, `:74-94`); `essentia_output` (the Emptying Transfuser) advertises no suction, draws a matching unit from sources within sixteen blocks of its facing direction through the handler's two-phase drain-and-confirm path, and hands it to the connected device (`common/tiles/essentia/TileEssentiaOutput.java:20-33`, `:49-52`, `:74-94`). Both refuse to store anything themselves (their add calls return the whole amount: `common/tiles/essentia/TileEssentiaInput.java:69-72`, `common/tiles/essentia/TileEssentiaOutput.java:69-72`), and the tree's text describes the pair as the pipe-to-jar and jar-to-pipe bridges (`assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1371`).
16. **The handler's cached source list is fail-closed and rebuilds itself.** Per-caller lists are distance-ordered but dropped wholesale — with ten seconds of no re-search — the moment a cached coordinate resolves to nothing or to a non-source, and an empty spatial scan suppresses the same way (`common/lib/events/EssentiaHandler.java:22-24`, `:41-61`, `:278-285`); a per-tile hook removes one entry (`:283-285`). Blocked sources are skipped everywhere in that file, which is the transport meaning of the sealed jar in item 11 ([fnd-04-evidence.md](fnd-04-evidence.md) item 21).
17. **Breakage costs aura, and reload restores devices but not searches.** Breaking a loaded tube converts its contents to aura pollution (`common/blocks/essentia/BlockTube.java:212-226`) and the shared transport spill then runs again over the same tile (`common/blocks/BlockTCTile.java:54-65`, enabled by default at `:73-76`), so the written cost of a loaded pipe may be double its contents — rank-1 checkable (T8). The transport tiles persist their state through the shared save and sync path (`common/tiles/essentia/TileTube.java:61-103`, `common/tiles/essentia/TileTubeBuffer.java:40-67`, `common/tiles/essentia/TileJarFillable.java:32-52`, `common/tiles/devices/TileMirrorEssentia.java:39-58`) while the handler caches live only in memory (`common/lib/events/EssentiaHandler.java:287-295`), so a reload brings every device back as it was and rebuilds the searches from scratch.

## Rank-1 capture checklist

Reference setup for every step: the clean 1.12.2 profile whose only mods are the BETA26 jar and Baubles (`REF-0002` and the "Environment feasibility" section of [reference-catalog.md](reference-catalog.md)); record the profile, world seed, and game mode in a new `REF` entry before starting. The established session world is **creative**, which supplies the tube family, the transfusers, jars, alembics, and both mirror types from the mod's creative tab without research (every Thaumcraft block sets that tab, `common/blocks/BlockTC.java:20`), so the survival recipes behind the research pages `TUBES` and `ESSENTIATRANSPORT` are irrelevant here; research grants, if used at all, happen between measurements, never during one. Carry a Caster, an Essentia Resonator (the in-game suction and contents readout, `assets/thaumcraft/lang/en_us.lang:1379`), jars, phials, labels, bellows, and mirror items as the tools of these steps.

- **T1 — One hop and one unit.** Fill a source jar, then build a one-hop line that ends at an empty jar: the receiving jar must sit beneath the tube that feeds it (the jar's only transport face is its top), and the source is any device a tube can drain — a jar beneath its own tube, or an alembic beside a tube on any face but its labelled one and its underside (items 3-5, 11). Record the resonator's suction reading on each segment, the units that arrive per ten seconds, and that no tube ever shows more than one unit at rest.
- **T2 — Filter marking.** Put a filter tube mid-line carrying two aspects from two sources; mark it with a phial of one aspect and record that only that aspect moves, then clear it by sneak click and record the filter disappearing (items 3, 9).
- **T3 — Valve cut, hand and redstone.** With the line flowing, close the valve by hand, then with a redstone signal, recording where flow stops; then close it while flow is live and read the resonator on the valve across the next few ticks — the source predicts its advertised suction drops to zero at the next scan, so record whether any unit crosses or lingers in the transient window before the line goes quiet (item 7).
- **T4 — Directional tube.** Rotate a directional tube with the caster and record the working orientation; swap source and sink and confirm nothing moves (item 8).
- **T5 — Restricted tube.** Run the same source-sink pair over a fixed chain with and without a restricted segment, recording units per interval and the resonator's values, so the halving is visible or absent (item 8).
- **T6 — Buffer numbers.** Fill a buffer from one aspect-rich source until it refuses, counting the units the goggles or a resonator shows (the source says ten; the in-game text says eight); add one, two, and three bellows and record the suction and the resulting pull; cycle a side's choke and record the blue and red bands against the suction readings; read the comparator at part and full fill (item 10).
- **T7 — Congestion.** Aim two differently typed pulls at one junction and record the venting: sound, particle colour, duration, and whether any essentia is lost; then remove one pull and record the line resuming (item 6).
- **T8 — Mirrors, reload, and breakage.** Link a mirror pair across a reasonably long distance (the link is item-based with no range cap; the eight in item 13 is the handler's search radius around a partner mirror, not a link limit), put a source jar beside one mirror and a receiving jar beside the other, send a jar's worth of essentia through, and record the instability build-up and any aura or flux change; unload or leave the partner chunk and record the link going stale and recovering; finally break a tube holding a unit and a sealed jar holding essentia and compare the aura cost (items 11-14, 17).
